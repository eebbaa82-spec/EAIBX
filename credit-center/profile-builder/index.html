Credit Management Center

Borrower profile builder

Prepare the profile a bank or MFI will ask for: who you are, what you need, your finances, banking record and collateral. Get a readiness check, the exact documents for your loan type, and a bank-ready PDF with your application letter once your business identity is verified. Your profile answers stay on your device.

Confirm who is applying

The bank-ready pack is only for the business itself or someone it has authorized. Give the business's TIN and licence and your own details; Investopia checks them against the Ministry of Trade register and sends you a verification code. You can fill in the rest of the profile meanwhile.

Check the business on the Ministry of Trade register

  1. Open etrade.et and find “Search Existing Business”.
  2. Enter the TIN or the business licence number.
  3. Check that the name matches and the licence is valid or renewed for this year.

Your answers stay on this device. Only the identity check details are sent to Investopia; nothing is sent to any lender.

Want a second pair of eyes before you apply?

Investopia credit advisors can review your profile and help you prepare for the lender's questions.
