Credit Management Center

Better credit relationships between businesses and lenders.

The Erudite Africa Connect Credit Management Center of Excellence helps businesses manage their relationships with banks and microfinance institutions (MFIs), and helps banks and MFIs carry out faster, better-documented client due diligence.
I'm a businessI'm a bank or MFI

For businesses

Be the borrower every lender wants.

Getting credit is only the start. Keeping a strong relationship with your bank or MFI means clear records, on-time reporting and early, honest conversations. Erudite Africa Connect gives you the tools to do all three.

  • Credit profile. Keep your financial statements, licenses, collateral documents, existing facilities and repayment record in one secure profile, ready to share with any lender you choose.

  • Facility tracker. See every loan, overdraft and guarantee in one place, with repayment dates, covenants and renewal deadlines, and get reminders before they are due.

  • Lender matching. Find banks and MFIs whose products fit your size, sector and need, from working capital and term loans to trade finance and leasing.

  • Relationship support. Prepare for credit committee questions, facility renewals and restructuring requests with guides, templates and a credit advisor when you need one.

  • Early warning. If repayments or covenants come under pressure, Erudite Africa Connect flags it early and connects you with Business Recovery support before the problem grows.

For banks and MFIs

Faster due diligence. Healthier portfolios.

Erudite Africa Connect gives credit teams a secure, consent-based way to collect, review and verify client information, and to follow borrowers after disbursement.

  • Client due diligence workspace. Request, receive and review know-your-customer (KYC) and know-your-business (KYB) documents through a secure data room, with a standard checklist your team can adapt to its own policies and regulatory requirements.

  • Verified business data. With the client's consent, see their EHIRA grade, diagnostic report and financial summaries, reviewed by Erudite Africa Connect analysts.

  • Structured appraisal support. Receive applications in a consistent format, with financial statements spread into standard ratios, so analysts spend less time on data entry and more on judgment.

  • Follow-up and monitoring. Track covenants, document expiry dates, site visits and repayment behavior for connected borrowers, with early warning alerts.

  • Pre-screened deal flow. Receive referrals of businesses from the Erudite Africa Connect marketplace and programs that match your lending criteria.

  • Staff training and certification. Train and certify your credit teams at our sister service, the Erudite Africa Center of Excellence.

For loan officers and credit analysts

Spread up to five periods of financial statements in the standard financial analysis form and get the totals, trend and analytical ratios at once. Add the loan application details, sector and regional context from official sources and your own findings and documents, and print a credit analysis report in the loan application annex format.

How it works

  1. 1

    The business builds its credit profile

    and completes the EHIRA assessment.

  2. 2

    The business chooses which lenders

    may see its profile.

  3. 3

    The lender reviews and requests

    any further documents through the secure workspace.

  4. 4

    Both sides track the facility

    after disbursement, with shared reminders and early warnings.

Businesses decide which lenders see their information. Lenders see only what the business shares, and every access is logged.

Erudite Africa Connect is not a lender or a credit reference bureau. Every credit decision is made by the bank or MFI under its own policies and the applicable National Bank of Ethiopia directives.
