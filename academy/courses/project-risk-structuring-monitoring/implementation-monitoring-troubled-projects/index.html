Implementation monitoring and troubled projects

Module 04 / 5 · 60 min

Read progress against money spent, catch overruns and delays while they are small, and restructure a troubled project on forward-looking numbers rather than sunk costs.

In this module

  1. 1.Follow-up during implementation
  2. 2.Follow-up after start-up
  3. 3.Progress against spending
  4. 4.Warning signs in projects
  5. 5.Restructuring a troubled project
  6. 6.Case study: Sebeta one year late and over budget
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • State the objectives of follow-up during implementation and after start-up
  • Compare physical progress with spending and estimate cost at completion
  • Recognize the warning signs of a troubled project
  • Design a restructuring package on forward-looking cash flows and route it to the right authority
