Economic, environmental, social and regulatory appraisal

Module 02 / 5 · 55 min

Look beyond the project's own profit and loss to the risks that can close a profitable plant: pollution, community conflict, foreign exchange and changing regulation.

In this module

  1. 1.Economic appraisal: good for the country, not only the owners
  2. 2.Sebeta's economic and foreign exchange profile

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how economic appraisal differs from financial appraisal
  • Measure a project's foreign exchange earning or saving
  • Apply the environmental and social checks a bank requires for large project loans
  • Identify foreign exchange, incentive and regulatory dependencies and set conditions for them
