Introduction to Financial Management

Module 02 / 6 · 60 min

Understand how money flows through a small business, the difference between revenue, costs, expenses and profit, why cash is not profit, and what owners are responsible for.

In this module

  1. 1.What financial management means for a small business
  2. 2.Understanding money flow
  3. 3.Revenue, costs, expenses and profit
  4. 4.Why cash is not profit
  5. 5.Fixed and variable costs, and break-even
  6. 6.Financial responsibilities of owners and managers
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the financial management cycle of plan, record, control and review
  • Trace operating, investing and financing money flows in a small business
  • Calculate a simple profit estimate and a break-even level of sales
  • Explain why a cash balance differs from profit and list the owners' financial responsibilities
