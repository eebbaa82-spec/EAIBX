Basic Bookkeeping Principles

Module 03 / 6 · 60 min

Learn the bookkeeping cycle, the source documents and vouchers behind every entry, how to build a chart of accounts, and how long and how well Ethiopian law expects you to keep records.

In this module

  1. 1.The bookkeeping cycle
  2. 2.Source documents
  3. 3.Receipts and vouchers
  4. 4.The chart of accounts
  5. 5.Record-keeping fundamentals and how long to keep records
  6. 6.Financial reporting rules for SMEs
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Follow the bookkeeping cycle from source document to monthly summary
  • Identify the main source documents and what a valid tax invoice must show
  • Prepare receipt, payment, petty cash and internal vouchers, including for a missing receipt
  • Build a numbered chart of accounts and apply Ethiopian record-keeping and financial reporting rules
