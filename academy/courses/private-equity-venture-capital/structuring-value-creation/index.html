Structuring for protection and creating value

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Choose instruments and rights that protect the downside in Ethiopia, then build the 100-day plan and the governance, finance and add-on moves that grow value.

In this module

  1. 1.Common, preference and convertible instruments
  2. 2.Liquidation preference: who is paid first
  3. 3.Anti-dilution protection
  4. 4.Offshore holding structures and FX protections
  5. 5.The 100-day plan and governance upgrades

  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare common shares, preference shares and convertible instruments for a PE or VC deal
  • Calculate the effect of liquidation preferences and anti-dilution clauses
  • Explain offshore holding structures and FX protections within Ethiopian rules
  • Design a 100-day plan, governance upgrades, finance professionalization and add-on acquisitions
