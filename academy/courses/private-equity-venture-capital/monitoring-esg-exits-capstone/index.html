Portfolio monitoring, ESG, exits and the capstone IC

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Monitor investments through KPIs, board work and ESG standards, plan the exit and FX repatriation, and present the full Batu IC paper.

In this module

  1. 1.KPIs and board work
  2. 2.ESG: IFC Performance Standards, IRIS+ and the 2X Criteria
  3. 3.Exit routes in Ethiopia
  4. 4.Preparing for exit and the ESX route
  5. 5.Planning FX repatriation of exit proceeds
  6. 6.Capstone: the Batu investment committee
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Design a KPI pack and a board routine to monitor a portfolio company
  • Apply IFC Performance Standards, IRIS+ and the 2X Criteria to monitoring and impact reporting
  • Compare exit routes in Ethiopia, including trade sale, secondary sale, ESX listing and buybacks
  • Plan FX repatriation of exit proceeds and defend a full IC paper in the capstone
