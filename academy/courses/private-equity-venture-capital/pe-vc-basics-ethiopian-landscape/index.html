PE and VC basics and the Ethiopian landscape

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

How private equity and venture capital funds are built, paid and measured, and who invests in Ethiopia under which rules.

In this module

  1. 1.Private capital as an asset class
  2. 2.The fund: general partner and limited partners
  3. 3.Fees, carried interest and the waterfall
  4. 4.The J-curve and how returns are measured
  5. 5.Who invests in Ethiopia
  6. 6.The rules and the constraints
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the PE and VC asset class and the stages from seed to buyout
  • Describe a GP/LP fund structure, management fees, carried interest and the distribution waterfall
  • Read the J-curve and the main return measures: IRR, MOIC, DPI, RVPI and TVPI
  • Describe the Ethiopian PE and VC landscape, the laws that frame it and its main constraints
