Sourcing, screening, valuation and term sheets

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Find and screen deals, value a young or growing company, build a cap table and read a term sheet in plain language.

In this module

  1. 1.Sourcing deal flow
  2. 2.Investment criteria and the screening case
  3. 3.First meetings and the investment memorandum outline
  4. 4.The VC method of valuation
  5. 5.Pre-money, post-money, dilution and cap tables
  6. 6.Term sheets in plain language
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build deal flow and screen opportunities against clear investment criteria
  • Run a first meeting and outline an investment memorandum
  • Value an early-stage company with the VC method and explain pre-money, post-money and dilution
  • Build a simple cap table and explain the key terms of a VC or PE term sheet
