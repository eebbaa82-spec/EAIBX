Valuing shares, corporate actions and the Bank A capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Value a listed Ethiopian bank with dividend discount and price-to-book methods, work out what rights and bonus issues do to holders, and defend a buy, hold or sell view.

In this module

  1. 1.The dividend discount model
  2. 2.Cost of equity and the two-stage model
  3. 3.Relative value: price-to-book and price-to-earnings
  4. 4.Rights issues and the value of a right
  5. 5.Bonus shares, splits, buybacks and mergers
  6. 6.Capstone: buy, hold or sell Bank A
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Value a share with the constant-growth and two-stage dividend discount models
  • Estimate a cost of equity for an Ethiopian bank from T-bill yields and a risk premium
  • Value a bank on price-to-book and price-to-earnings multiples and a justified P/B
  • Calculate the theoretical ex-rights price, the value of a right and the effect of a bonus issue
  • Combine equity and bond analysis into a buy, hold or sell recommendation
