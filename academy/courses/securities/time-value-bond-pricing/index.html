Time value, bond pricing, credit spreads and duration

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Move to the advanced level: discount cash flows, price bonds clean and dirty, solve for yield to maturity, build a yield curve from T-bill auctions, price a credit spread and measure price risk with duration and convexity.

In this module

  1. 1.Present value, future value and compounding
  2. 2.Annuities and uneven cash flows
  3. 3.Bond price and yield to maturity
  4. 4.Clean price, dirty price and accrued interest
  5. 5.Building a yield curve from T-bill auctions
  6. 6.Credit spreads, structure and sukuk
  7. 7.Duration and convexity
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute present and future values and annuities under different compounding conventions
  • Price a bond as the present value of its cash flows and solve for yield to maturity
  • Separate the clean price from accrued interest and the dirty price
  • Build a yield curve from T-bill auctions and build up a credit spread from risk, structure and liquidity
  • Calculate Macaulay duration, modified duration and convexity and estimate price changes
