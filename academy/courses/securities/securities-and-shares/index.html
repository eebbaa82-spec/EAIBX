What a security is, and the shares you can hold

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Learn what makes an instrument a security under Ethiopian law, how it is held and transferred, and what ordinary and preference shares give their owners.

In this module

  1. 1.What a security is
  2. 2.Registered, certificated and dematerialised securities
  3. 3.Ordinary and preference shares
  4. 4.Par value, market value and dividend yield
  5. 5.Shareholder rights and who may hold bank shares
  6. 6.Ethiopia's existing pool of shares
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what a security is under the Capital Market Proclamation and why transferability matters
  • Distinguish registered, certificated and dematerialised holdings and the role of the central securities depository
  • Compare ordinary and preference shares and the rights each carries
  • Calculate a dividend yield and explain the difference between par value and market value
