← Financial Modeling and Company Valuation

DCF and multiples valuation

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Value a company from its free cash flow, a cost of capital and a terminal value, bridge to equity per share, and cross-check with P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/B when Ethiopian comparables are scarce.

In this module

  1. 1.Free cash flow to the firm
  2. 2.Discounting at WACC
  3. 3.Terminal value
  4. 4.From enterprise value to equity per share
  5. 5.P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/B
  6. 6.Choosing peers when few Ethiopian comparables exist
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute free cash flow to the firm from a three-statement forecast
  • Discount cash flows at WACC and estimate a terminal value with the growing perpetuity method
  • Bridge enterprise value to equity value and value per share
  • Apply P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/B and choose peers sensibly when few Ethiopian comparables exist
