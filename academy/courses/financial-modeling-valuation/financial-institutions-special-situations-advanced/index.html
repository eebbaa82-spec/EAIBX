← Financial Modeling and Company Valuation

Banks, special situations, reporting and advanced models

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Value banks and insurers within capital rules, handle minority, control and privatization questions, present a valuation report, and take the first steps into merger models, simulation and model review.

In this module

  1. 1.Valuing banks and insurers
  2. 2.Capital constraints on bank dividends
  3. 3.Minority, control, unlisted discounts and privatization
  4. 4.The valuation report and football field
  5. 5.Advanced: merger models, accretion and dilution
  6. 6.Advanced: Monte Carlo, model review and expert valuation
  7. 7.Capstone: Ethio telecom in birr and USD
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Value banks and insurers with dividend discount and excess return models, respecting SBB/95/2025 capital ratios
  • Apply minority, control and unlisted share discounts and sum-of-the-parts valuation to special situations such as privatization
  • Present a valuation report with a football field and sensitivities that meets prospectus and committee expectations
  • Explain accretion and dilution, Monte Carlo simulation and independent model review at an advanced level
