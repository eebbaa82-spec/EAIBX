Scenarios, inflation, FX and the Ethiopian cost of capital

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Build flexible scenario-driven models in birr and USD, keep inflation, exchange rates and WACC consistent after the 2024 float, and defend an Ethiopian cost of capital.

In this module

  1. 1.Scenario switches, data tables and sensitivities
  2. 2.Circularity and model audit
  3. 3.Nominal versus real modeling
  4. 4.Currency of cash flows and FX paths after the 2024 float
  5. 5.Three FX scenarios and their consistency with WACC
  6. 6.Estimating the Ethiopian cost of capital
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Add scenario switches, data tables and circularity controls, and audit a model systematically
  • Model in nominal or real terms and in birr or USD with consistent inflation and FX assumptions
  • Build exchange rate paths under the market-based regime and reflect FXD/01/2024 conversion and repatriation rules
  • Estimate a defensible Ethiopian cost of equity and WACC with country risk, currency, size and illiquidity premiums
