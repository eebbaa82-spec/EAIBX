← Financial Modeling and Company Valuation

Historical analysis and forecasting

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Read the past with ratios and normalized earnings, then forecast revenue, costs, working capital, capex, tax and debt from clear drivers.

In this module

  1. 1.Common-size statements and ratio analysis
  2. 2.Normalizing earnings
  3. 3.Forecasting revenue and costs from drivers
  4. 4.Working capital in days
  5. 5.Capex, depreciation, tax and the debt schedule
  6. 6.The assumption book and forecast sanity checks
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Prepare common-size statements and the key profitability, efficiency and leverage ratios
  • Normalize earnings for one-off items such as the foreign exchange losses that followed the 2024 float
  • Forecast revenue from volume and price drivers and costs from their structure
  • Forecast working capital in days, capex and depreciation, tax at the 30% corporate rate and a debt schedule
