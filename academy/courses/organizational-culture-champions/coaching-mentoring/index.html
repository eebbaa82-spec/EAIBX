Coaching and Mentoring Others

Module 05 / 6 · 60 min

Help colleagues grow through coaching conversations, peer support and the deliberate transfer of knowledge from experienced staff.

In this module

  1. 1.Coaching fundamentals: coaching, mentoring and managing
  2. 2.The GROW coaching conversation
  3. 3.Listening, questions and feedback
  4. 4.Peer support and buddy systems
  5. 5.Knowledge transfer
  6. 6.Building a coaching culture and measuring impact
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Distinguish coaching, mentoring and managing, and choose the right one
  • Run a coaching conversation with the GROW model, good questions and SBI feedback
  • Set up peer support and buddy systems that help new and struggling colleagues
  • Plan the transfer of critical knowledge from experienced staff and measure the results
