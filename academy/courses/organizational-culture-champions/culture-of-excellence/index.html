Building a Culture of Excellence

Module 02 / 6 · 60 min

Build the daily habits, quality standards and improvement routines that make excellent work normal rather than heroic.

In this module

  1. 1.What a culture of excellence looks like
  2. 2.High-performance habits
  3. 3.Quality orientation
  4. 4.The cost of poor quality
  5. 5.Continuous improvement culture
  6. 6.Recognizing and sustaining excellence
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe what a culture of excellence looks like in observable standards and measures
  • Introduce high-performance habits such as daily huddles, visible goals and reliable follow-through
  • Apply quality principles and calculate the cost of poor quality
  • Lead continuous improvement with PDCA, root cause analysis and staff suggestions
