Becoming a Culture Ambassador

Module 06 / 6 · 70 min

Diagnose culture with evidence, drive engagement, write a Culture Improvement Action Plan and sustain the change by building it into everyday systems.

In this module

  1. 1.Diagnosing your culture with evidence
  2. 2.Prioritizing what to fix first
  3. 3.Writing the Culture Improvement Action Plan
  4. 4.Driving engagement
  5. 5.Sustaining culture
  6. 6.The 90-day review
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Diagnose workplace culture using surveys, eNPS, turnover, absenteeism and operational data
  • Prioritize the few culture gaps that matter most and link them to business results
  • Write a Culture Improvement Action Plan with values-to-behaviors, actions, owners and measures
  • Drive engagement and sustain culture through systems, rituals and a 90-day review
