The rules and the campus capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Apply fair-marketing, data consent and disinformation rules, then build a one-week content plan and three compliant posts promoting the bank's mobile app to university students.

In this module

  1. 1.Fair marketing rules
  2. 2.Avoiding misleading claims
  3. 3.Comments, rumours and disinformation
  4. 4.Capstone: the one-week campus content plan
  5. 5.Capstone: three posts and the portfolio
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the main Ethiopian rules on fair advertising, data consent and online content to bank marketing
  • Recognise misleading claims and fix them
  • Handle comments, rumours and disinformation about the bank calmly and lawfully
  • Produce a one-week content plan and three compliant posts for university students
  • Assemble a content portfolio for assessment
