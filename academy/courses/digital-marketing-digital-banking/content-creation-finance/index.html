← Digital Marketing and Digital Banking

Creating clear, compliant content

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Write copy, design visuals and make short videos that explain bank products, rates and fees clearly in Amharic, English and other local languages.

In this module

  1. 1.Copywriting for finance
  2. 2.Visuals and short video
  3. 3.Writing in local languages
  4. 4.Explaining rates and fees clearly
  5. 5.The pre-publish checklist
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Write short, plain copy for a financial product with one message and one call to action
  • Design visuals and short videos that work on small screens and low data
  • Adapt content into Amharic, Afaan Oromo and other languages without losing meaning
  • Explain interest, fees and the total cost of a product clearly and honestly
  • Check content against a simple compliance checklist before publishing
