Digital channels in Ethiopia

Module 02 / 5 · 55 min

Know what each channel does best, from Facebook, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn to SMS and USSD push, websites, app stores and influencers, and how to combine them.

In this module

  1. 1.Facebook, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn
  2. 2.Running an official Telegram channel safely
  3. 3.SMS and USSD push
  4. 4.Website, landing pages and app store listings
  5. 5.Influencers and student ambassadors
  6. 6.Choosing the channel mix
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the strengths and limits of Facebook, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn for bank marketing
  • Use SMS and USSD push with consent and within the bank's rules
  • Explain the role of the bank's website and app store listing in conversion
  • Choose and brief an influencer or student ambassador responsibly
  • Match channels to an audience and a goal
