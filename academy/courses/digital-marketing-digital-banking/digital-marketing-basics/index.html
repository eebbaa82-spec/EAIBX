Digital marketing basics and the Ethiopian audience

Module 01 / 5 · 50 min · Free preview

Learn the digital marketing funnel from awareness to advocacy, the difference between owned, earned and paid media, and who the Ethiopian digital customer really is.

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the five stages of the digital marketing funnel and what a bank wants at each stage
  • Tell owned, earned and paid media apart and give a bank example of each
  • Describe the Ethiopian digital audience: mobile-first, data-cost conscious, multilingual and social-media led
  • Read simple funnel numbers and spot where customers drop out
