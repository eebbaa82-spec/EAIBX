Disclosure, stewardship and the capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Write ESG risk factors and ISSB-aligned disclosures, monitor and engage after closing, and bring the course together in the Entoto Valley structuring proposal.

In this module

  1. 1.ESG risk factors in a prospectus
  2. 2.ISSB-aligned and impact reporting
  3. 3.Post-deal monitoring and active ownership
  4. 4.Controversies and reputational events
  5. 5.Capstone: structuring Entoto Valley's sustainability-linked loan
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Draft specific ESG risk factors for a prospectus or information memorandum
  • Outline ISSB-aligned and impact reporting for a client and check it for consistency
  • Plan post-deal monitoring, active ownership and controversy response
  • Structure and defend a sustainability-linked loan with an integrated ESG valuation

Built on

  • Erudite Africa course specification 3.k: ESG Integration in Investment Banking, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • Erudite Africa Training Module: Ethiopian Context, programme overview and regulatory register (October 2026)
  • NBE Directive No. SBB/91/2024 (Bank Corporate Governance); ECMA Directive No. 1030/2024 (reported); Environmental Impact Assessment Proclamation No. 299/2002, Environmental Pollution Control Proclamation No. 300/2002 and Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019, as cited in the course specification
  • World Bank Group: Ethiopia Country Climate and Development Report (2024); A Review of Industrial Parks in Ethiopia (2022); Making Ethiopia's Financial Sector Work for Women (2024)
  • IFC Performance Standards, Equator Principles (EP4), ICMA Green, Social and Sustainability-Linked Bond Principles, loan market sustainability-linked loan principles, SASB, ISSB IFRS S1 and S2, TCFD and UN PRI (general market practice)

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
