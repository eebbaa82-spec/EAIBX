ESG in valuation and credit analysis

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Bring ESG risks and opportunities into cash flows, discount rates and scenarios without double counting, and show their effect on value and debt capacity.

In this module

  1. 1.Where ESG enters the numbers
  2. 2.Cash flows or discount rate?
  3. 3.Scenario analysis for climate and social risk
  4. 4.ESG in credit analysis and debt capacity
  5. 5.Opportunities: valuing the upside honestly
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Translate ESG findings into specific cash flow lines: capex, opex, revenue at risk and downtime
  • Explain when to adjust the discount rate and why adjusting both rate and cash flows double counts
  • Build probability-weighted scenarios for climate and social risk and read their effect on enterprise value
  • Show how ESG risks change debt capacity and covenant headroom in credit analysis
