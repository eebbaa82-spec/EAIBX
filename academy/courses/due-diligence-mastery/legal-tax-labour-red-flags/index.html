Legal, regulatory, tax and labour DD and Ethiopian red flags

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Verify ownership, licences, contracts and land rights, estimate tax and labour exposures, and recognise the red flags that recur in Ethiopian company records.

In this module

  1. 1.Incorporation, share register and governance records
  2. 2.Licences, contracts and litigation
  3. 3.Land, lease and title deeds
  4. 4.Tax due diligence
  5. 5.Labour due diligence
  6. 6.Red flags in Ethiopian company records
  7. 7.Exercise: the Hawassa data room top ten

In this module you will learn to

  • Verify incorporation, the share register, licences, material contracts and litigation
  • Test land, lease and title rights for each important property
  • Identify and estimate tax and labour exposures
  • Recognise common red flags in Ethiopian company records and rank the top issues in a data room
