Purpose, scope and planning of due diligence

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Decide what kind of due diligence a deal needs, set materiality and scope, and run the request list, data room, advisers and Q&A to a timetable.

In this module

  1. 1.Why due diligence matters in Ethiopian deals
  2. 2.Buy-side, vendor, lender and IPO due diligence
  3. 3.Materiality and scope
  4. 4.Checklists and request lists
  5. 5.Organising and reviewing a data room
  6. 6.Planning the exercise: advisers, budget and timeline
  7. 7.Managing Q&A and management meetings
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the purpose of buy-side, vendor, lender and IPO due diligence and who performs each
  • Set materiality and a scope that follow the deal's key risks
  • Build a request list and organise a data room review
  • Plan the budget, advisers, timeline and Q&A process of a multi-disciplinary due diligence
