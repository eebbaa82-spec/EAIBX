Financial due diligence and quality of earnings

Module 02 / 5 · 70 min

Turn reported profit into sustainable earnings, prove it to cash, and find the net debt, debt-like items and loan book losses that the balance sheet does not show.

In this module

  1. 1.Quality of earnings: what is sustainable?
  2. 2.Normalisations in Ethiopian companies
  3. 3.Revenue recognition and the cash proof
  4. 4.Inflation and exchange rate effects
  5. 5.Net debt and debt-like items
  6. 6.Working capital and off-balance-sheet items
  7. 7.Financial DD of a lender: re-grading the loan book
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Prepare a quality of earnings bridge from reported to normalised profit
  • Test revenue recognition and prove reported sales to cash and VAT returns
  • Separate inflation and exchange rate effects from real performance
  • Identify net debt, debt-like items, working capital and off-balance-sheet exposures
  • Re-grade a lender's loan book and quantify the provision shortfall

  • Ethiopian tax laws: Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016 as amended by No. 1395/2025, VAT Proclamation No. 1341/2024, Federal Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016, Transfer Pricing Directive No. 981/2024
  • ESX Rulebook (December 2024)

  • IFC Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability
