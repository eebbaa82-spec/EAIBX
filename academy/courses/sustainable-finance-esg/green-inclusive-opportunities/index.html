Green and inclusive lending opportunities

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Recognize and size the green and inclusive business Ethiopian banks can grow: off-grid solar, clean cooking, climate-smart agriculture, green buildings, e-mobility and finance for women and youth.

In this module

  1. 1.A map of green lending in Ethiopia
  2. 2.Off-grid solar and clean cooking: affordability first
  3. 3.Climate-smart agriculture
  4. 4.Green buildings and e-mobility
  5. 5.Finance for women and youth
  6. 6.Green does not mean risk-free
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Identify the main green lending segments in Ethiopia and their typical borrowers
  • Assess the affordability of a green product with a simple cash flow comparison
  • Explain why women and youth finance is both an inclusion goal and a business opportunity
  • Spot the risks that come with green products and how to manage them
