What sustainable finance and ESG mean

Module 01 / 5 · 45 min · Free preview

Learn the language of sustainable finance, ESG, E&S risk, the SDGs and double materiality, and why it makes business sense for an Ethiopian bank.

In this module

  1. 1.Sustainable finance in one sentence
  2. 2.E&S risk is credit risk in disguise
  3. 3.Double materiality: two directions of impact
  4. 4.The SDGs and Ethiopia's green strategies

  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Define sustainable finance, ESG and environmental and social (E&S) risk in plain words
  • Explain double materiality: how the world affects the bank and how the bank affects the world
  • Link bank lending to the Sustainable Development Goals and Ethiopia's green strategies
  • Make the business case for E&S risk management in Birr terms
