Ethiopia's climate and social exposure

Module 02 / 5 · 55 min

Understand the climate and social risks Ethiopia faces and trace how drought, floods, power cuts and displacement reach a bank's loan book.

In this module

  1. 1.Ethiopia's main climate hazards
  2. 2.What the World Bank's climate report says
  3. 3.Physical risk and transition risk
  4. 4.How a climate shock reaches the balance sheet
  5. 5.When the power goes out
  6. 6.Social exposure: displacement, land and livelihoods
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe Ethiopia's main physical climate hazards and the sectors most exposed
  • Distinguish physical risk from transition risk with Ethiopian examples
  • Trace how a climate shock moves from a borrower to the bank's provisions and capital
  • Build a simple hazard and sensitivity score for parts of a loan portfolio
