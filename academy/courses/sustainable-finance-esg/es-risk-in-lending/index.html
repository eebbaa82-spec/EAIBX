E&S risk in lending: screen, categorize, condition

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Apply the core E&S steps of a bank's management system to real loan applications: the exclusion list, screening, risk categories and E&S conditions.

In this module

  1. 1.The E&S process inside the credit cycle
  2. 2.The exclusion list: activities the bank will not finance
  3. 3.Categories A, B and C
  4. 4.Ethiopian law and the IFC Performance Standards
  5. 5.Writing E&S conditions that work
  6. 6.Screening practice: first five applications
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Screen a loan application against an exclusion list and national law
  • Assign an E&S risk category (A, B or C) and explain the reasons
  • Draft proportionate E&S conditions for approval, disbursement and monitoring
  • Explain how the EIA Proclamation and the IFC Performance Standards apply to bank clients

  • World Bank Group: Ethiopia Country Climate and Development Report (2024) and Making Ethiopia's Financial Sector Work for Women (2024)
