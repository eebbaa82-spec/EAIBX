Anti-corruption, whistleblowing and the dilemma cards

Module 05 / 5 · 60 min

Recognize bribery and kickbacks in lending and procurement, link corruption to money laundering, use safe reporting channels, and work through all four of Bank A's dilemma cards with a board-level governance check.

In this module

  1. 1.What corruption looks like
  2. 2.Kickbacks and red flags in lending
  3. 3.Card 4: a kickback in procurement
  4. 4.Corruption and money laundering
  5. 5.Speaking up safely
  6. 6.Capstone: the four cards and a board self-check
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Define bribery, kickbacks and related corruption offences in plain terms
  • Spot red flags of corruption in lending, collateral valuation and procurement
  • Explain how corruption links to money laundering reporting duties
  • Use whistleblowing channels and explain how reporters are protected
  • Apply the whole course to the four dilemma cards and a board self-check
