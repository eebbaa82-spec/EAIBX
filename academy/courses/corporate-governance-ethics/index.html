Corporate Governance and Ethics

How Ethiopian boards and managers should govern, and how every staff member handles everyday ethical dilemmas

Learn who directs and checks an Ethiopian bank or company under SBB/91/2024, SBB/89/2024, SBB/88/2024, the Commercial Code and capital market rules, then work through four real-life dilemma cards on loans, gifts, data and kickbacks.

Start the course

0 of 5 modules completed

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the roles of shareholders, the board, management, auditors and regulators
  • Describe the main governance requirements for Ethiopian banks and listed companies
  • Apply the code of conduct to common dilemmas: gifts, conflicts of interest, confidentiality and whistleblowing
  • Recognize corruption risks in lending and procurement and report them through safe channels

The case you will follow

Bank A and the dilemma cards

Bank A is a fictional mid-sized Ethiopian commercial bank with Birr 120 billion of assets, about 14,000 shareholders and an 11-member board. Its compliance head, Hirut Desta, runs an ethics workshop built on four dilemma cards that follow relationship manager Meron Alemu and her colleagues: her cousin Yared asks for a loan; Wenji Coffee Exporters PLC offers a Meskel gift with cash while its renewal is pending; director Solomon Kebede asks for a client's data; and Lalibela Office Solutions PLC offers a 5% kickback on a branch fit-out. At board level, new company secretary Dawit Haile checks the board against SBB/91/2024, and a loan request from Rift Valley Steel PLC, owned by an influential shareholder, tests the related party rules. Each module plays one part, and the last module brings them together.

For
All staff of Ethiopian banks, microfinance institutions, insurers and companies (for the ethics modules), and managers, company secretaries and new directors (for the governance modules). No prerequisites.

The 5 modules

  1. Module 01Free previewWhat corporate governance isUnderstand why someone must watch the people who run a company, who those watchers are, and why governance failures hurt Ethiopian banks so badly.6 lessons · 50 min · quiz
  2. Module 02Governance structuresWalk through the general meeting, the board and its committees, the CEO, the company secretary and the auditors, and see how they fit together in an Ethiopian bank.6 lessons · 60 min · quiz
  3. Module 03Ethiopian governance requirementsApply the main Ethiopian rules: SBB/91/2024 and SBB/89/2024 for banks, the related party limits of SBB/88/2024, directors' duties under the Commercial Code, and listed company rules under the Capital Market Proclamation.6 lessons · 70 min · quiz
  4. Module 04Ethics in daily workUse the code of conduct to handle conflicts of interest, gifts and hospitality in the Ethiopian setting, confidentiality and inside information, through three of Bank A's dilemma cards.6 lessons · 60 min · quiz
  5. Module 05Anti-corruption, whistleblowing and the dilemma cardsRecognize bribery and kickbacks in lending and procurement, link corruption to money laundering, use safe reporting channels, and work through all four of Bank A's dilemma cards with a board-level governance check.6 lessons · 60 min · quiz
  6. Officer simulationA month as governance and ethics officer at Bank A head officeWork as governance and ethics officer: 11 real-life items, decisions with consequences and a hand-over note. Pass with 70% to qualify for the certificate.

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 4.a Corporate Governance and Ethics, Level 1 Beginner (October 2026)
  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/87/2024, SBB/88/2024, SBB/89/2024 and SBB/91/2024
  • Commercial Code of Ethiopia; Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025; NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025
  • ECMA Directive No. 980/2024 on capital market service providers
  • A commercial bank's Revised Credit Procedure Manual (August 2025), unnamed
  • G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance and the Basel Committee's corporate governance principles for banks
