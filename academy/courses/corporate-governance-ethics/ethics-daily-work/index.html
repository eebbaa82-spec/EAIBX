Ethics in daily work

Module 04 / 5 · 60 min

Use the code of conduct to handle conflicts of interest, gifts and hospitality in the Ethiopian setting, confidentiality and inside information, through three of Bank A's dilemma cards.

In this module

  1. 1.The code of conduct and the TRUST test
  2. 2.Conflicts of interest
  3. 3.Gifts and hospitality in Ethiopia
  4. 4.Keeping a gift register
  5. 5.Confidentiality and customer data
  6. 6.Inside information and personal trading
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what a code of conduct covers and use a simple test for ethical decisions
  • Recognize a conflict of interest and handle it by declaring and stepping back
  • Apply gift and hospitality rules to Ethiopian holidays and customs
  • Protect customer confidentiality and handle inside information correctly
