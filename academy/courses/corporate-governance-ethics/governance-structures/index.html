Governance structures

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Walk through the general meeting, the board and its committees, the CEO, the company secretary and the auditors, and see how they fit together in an Ethiopian bank.

In this module

  1. 1.The general meeting of shareholders
  2. 2.Who sits on a bank board
  3. 3.The four mandatory board committees
  4. 4.The CEO and senior management
  5. 5.The company secretary
  6. 6.Auditors and the three lines
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what the shareholders' general meeting decides
  • Describe how a bank board is composed and why some directors must be independent
  • Name the four mandatory board committees for Ethiopian banks and their main tasks
  • Describe the roles of the CEO, the company secretary, internal audit and the external auditor

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 4.a Corporate Governance and Ethics, Level 1 Beginner (October 2026)
  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/87/2024, SBB/88/2024, SBB/89/2024 and SBB/91/2024
  • Commercial Code of Ethiopia; Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025; NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025
  • ECMA Directive No. 980/2024 on capital market service providers
  • A commercial bank's Revised Credit Procedure Manual (August 2025), unnamed
  • G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance and the Basel Committee's corporate governance principles for banks
