What corporate governance is

Module 01 / 5 · 50 min · Free preview

Understand why someone must watch the people who run a company, who those watchers are, and why governance failures hurt Ethiopian banks so badly.

In this module

  1. 1.Who watches the people in charge?
  2. 2.The agency problem
  3. 3.Stakeholders and what they expect
  4. 4.The board steers, management rows
  5. 5.International principles: OECD and Basel
  6. 6.Why governance failures hurt Ethiopian banks
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Define corporate governance and explain the agency problem in plain words
  • Name the main stakeholders of an Ethiopian bank or company and what each expects
  • Separate the job of the board from the job of management
  • Describe the core ideas of the OECD and Basel governance principles
  • Explain how weak governance has harmed banks and why the NBE tightened the rules in 2024

  • Commercial Code of Ethiopia; Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025; NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025
  • ECMA Directive No. 980/2024 on capital market service providers
  • A commercial bank's Revised Credit Procedure Manual (August 2025), unnamed
  • G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance and the Basel Committee's corporate governance principles for banks
