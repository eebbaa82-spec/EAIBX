Banks and insurers: KPIs, models and valuation

Module 02 / 5 · 70 min

Build a balance-sheet-driven bank model with provisioning under SBB/90/2024 and capital under SBB/95/2025, value it with residual income, and read an insurer through its combined ratio, reserves and solvency.

In this module

  1. 1.How a bank makes money: NII, fees, cost of risk and capital
  2. 2.The balance-sheet-driven bank model
  3. 3.Cost of risk and provisioning under SBB/90/2024
  4. 4.The capital constraint under SBB/95/2025
  5. 5.Valuing a bank with residual income
  6. 6.Insurers: premiums, claims, combined ratio and solvency
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain NII, NIM, fees, cost of risk and capital as the drivers of bank value
  • Project a bank's balance sheet, provisions and capital ratios after the credit cap's removal
  • Value bank equity with a residual income model and interpret price to book
  • Analyze an insurer's premiums, loss and combined ratios, investment income and solvency
