Agro-processing, manufacturing, real estate and the sector report

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Model commodity price scenarios and seasonal working capital, factory utilization and import dependence, presale cash flows and RevPAR, then choose sector valuation methods and present the capstone report.

In this module

  1. 1.Agriculture and agro-processing: yields, prices and export FX
  2. 2.Seasonality and working capital in agro-processing
  3. 3.Manufacturing and industrial parks: utilization and import dependence
  4. 4.Real estate developers: presales and unit cash flows
  5. 5.Hospitality: occupancy, ADR and RevPAR
  6. 6.Choosing the valuation method and writing the sector report
  7. 7.Capstone: model and value Bank A or Operator B
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build commodity price scenarios and seasonal working capital for an agro-processor and exporter
  • Model capacity utilization, breakeven and import dependence for a manufacturer in an industrial park or SEZ
  • Project unit presale cash flows for a developer and occupancy, ADR and RevPAR for a hotel
  • Choose and justify sector valuation methods and write a sector investment report with Ethiopian scenarios
  • Deliver the capstone model and valuation for Bank A or Operator B
