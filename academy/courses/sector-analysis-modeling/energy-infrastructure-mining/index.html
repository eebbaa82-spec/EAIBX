Energy, infrastructure and mining models

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Model independent power producers from capacity, capacity factor and the PPA tariff, link to project finance metrics, assess the offtaker and indexation, and adapt the logic to mining.

In this module

  1. 1.The power sector framework and the PPP route
  2. 2.Capacity, capacity factor and the PPA tariff
  3. 3.Availability payments in infrastructure PPPs
  4. 4.Linking to the project model: CFADS, DSCR, indexation and FX
  5. 5.EEP offtaker risk and credit support
  6. 6.Mining models: reserves, grade, recovery and royalties
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Read the Ethiopian power sector's legal and PPP framework for its effect on project returns
  • Build energy yield and revenue from capacity, capacity factor and tariff, and compare energy and availability payments
  • Link the operating model to CFADS, DSCR and equity returns, with tariff indexation and FX
  • Assess offtaker payment risk and the structure of a mining model
