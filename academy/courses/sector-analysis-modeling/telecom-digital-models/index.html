Telecom and digital: subscribers, capex and mobile money

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Model a mobile operator from its subscriber funnel, ARPU and churn, carry FX-denominated network capex and licence costs, value mobile money, and test competition and FX scenarios.

In this module

  1. 1.The Ethiopian telecom market and its rules
  2. 2.Subscribers, ARPU and churn
  3. 3.Network capex, spectrum and FX-denominated costs
  4. 4.Mobile money economics: telebirr, M-PESA and the wallet business
  5. 5.Competition, FX and valuation of a challenger
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the structure and regulation of the Ethiopian telecom market and its effect on returns
  • Build a subscriber, ARPU and churn revenue model
  • Model network capex, spectrum and licence costs in foreign currency and test FX sensitivity
  • Estimate mobile money revenue and value a challenger operator under competition scenarios
