Non-deposit resources, performance and the three-year strategy

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Compare interbank, NBE facilities, bonds, subordinated debt, equity and development finance lines, manage mobilization with scorecards and campaign returns, and present Bank A's three-year strategy.

In this module

  1. 1.The interbank market and NBE standing facilities
  2. 2.Subordinated debt and corporate bonds
  3. 3.Equity, rights issues and new entrants
  4. 4.Development finance lines
  5. 5.Branch scorecards, KPIs and campaign return
  6. 6.Level 2 capstone: Bank A's three-year strategy
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare the cost, tenor and conditions of interbank borrowing, NBE standing facilities, bonds, subordinated debt, equity and DFI lines
  • Explain how capital rules and new entrants under SBB/94/2025 shape a bank's funding and capital plans
  • Build a branch scorecard and calculate the return on a mobilization campaign
  • Present a three-year resource mobilization strategy with targets consistent with lending plans and the LDR

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 1.b Resource Mobilization Strategies and Frameworks, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • NBE Directive NBE/INT/13/2026 and the NBE Monetary Policy Committee release No. 7 (13 July 2026)
  • NBE directives SBB/72/2019, SBB/94/2025, SBB/95/2025, FXD/01/2024 and FXD/04/2026, and National Bank of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1359/2025, as cited in the lessons
  • NBE Risk Management Guidelines (May 2010)
  • Erudite Africa Domestic Banking Operations course notes on deposit products and account maintenance (2026)
  • National Financial Inclusion Strategy II (2021 to 2025) and the NFIS-III refresh

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
