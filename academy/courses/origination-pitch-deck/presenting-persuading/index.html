Presenting and persuading boards and owners

Module 03 / 5 · 60 min

Present confidently to boards, owners and state panels, handle hard questions, respect cultural norms and prepare consistent Amharic and English materials.

In this module

  1. 1.The board-level presentation
  2. 2.Reading the room and cultural nuances
  3. 3.Handling hard questions
  4. 4.Amharic and English materials
  5. 5.Persuading owners, boards and committees
  6. 6.Rehearsal and feedback
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Structure a board-level presentation within a fixed time slot
  • Adapt style to family owners, bank and insurer boards, state panels and DFIs
  • Handle difficult questions calmly and accurately
  • Prepare Amharic and English materials that say the same thing
