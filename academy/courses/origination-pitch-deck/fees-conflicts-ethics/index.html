Fees, engagement letters, conflicts and ethics

Module 04 / 5 · 60 min

Structure and negotiate fees, write a sound engagement letter, run conflicts checks and stay within Ethiopian rules on confidentiality, inducements and marketing before registration.

In this module

  1. 1.Fee structures
  2. 2.Negotiating fees
  3. 3.The engagement letter
  4. 4.Conflicts checks
  5. 5.Confidential information
  6. 6.Inducements and gun-jumping
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Design a fee structure combining retainers, success fees and expenses
  • Negotiate fees without undermining the value of the advice
  • List the key terms of an engagement letter and why each matters
  • Run a conflicts check and manage confidential information
  • Recognize inducements and gun-jumping before ECMA registration
