← Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) Strategy and Execution

Structuring the deal and the legal documents

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Choose between asset and share deals, cash and shares, earn-outs, escrow and locked box or completion accounts, then turn the deal into a term sheet, SPA and shareholder agreement that protect each side.

In this module

  1. 1.Asset deals and share deals
  2. 2.Cash, shares, deferred payment and earn-outs
  3. 3.Locked box or completion accounts
  4. 4.Term sheet, SPA, warranties and indemnities
  5. 5.Shareholder agreements and minority governance
  6. 6.Financing the price in Ethiopian conditions
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare asset and share deals and the Ethiopian tax and transfer issues of each
  • Structure consideration with cash, shares, deferred payment, earn-outs and escrow
  • Choose between a locked box and completion accounts and compute the price adjustment
  • Draft the key protections: representations and warranties, indemnities, MAC clause and conditions precedent
  • Design governance terms in a shareholder agreement for a minority strategic investor
