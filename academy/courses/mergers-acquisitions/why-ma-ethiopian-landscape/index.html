Why companies merge and the Ethiopian deal landscape

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Separate deals that create value from deals that only create size, then map where Ethiopian M&A is happening now: bank consolidation, foreign entry, privatisation and family successions.

In this module

  1. 1.Horizontal, vertical and conglomerate deals
  2. 2.Growth, scale, capability and market entry
  3. 3.Where value comes from and why many deals fail
  4. 4.Bank consolidation after the ETB 5 billion rule
  5. 5.Foreign entry, privatisation and family successions
  6. 6.The running case: screening partners for Bank A
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Classify a deal as horizontal, vertical or conglomerate and name its strategic rationale
  • Explain how M&A creates value and the recurring reasons deals fail
  • Describe the forces driving Ethiopian deals after 2024: the ETB 5 billion bank capital rule, foreign entry, privatisation and succession
  • Frame the running case: Bank A's search for a merger partner
