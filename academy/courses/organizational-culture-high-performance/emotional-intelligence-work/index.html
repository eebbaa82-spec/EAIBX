Emotional Intelligence at Work

Module 06 / 6 · 55 min

Know your own emotions and triggers, manage your reactions under pressure, read others with empathy and handle relationships so the team stays calm, fair and effective.

In this module

  1. 1.What emotional intelligence is
  2. 2.Self-awareness: knowing yourself
  3. 3.Self-management: choosing your response
  4. 4.Social awareness: empathy and noticing stress
  5. 5.Relationship management
  6. 6.Putting emotional intelligence into daily practice
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the four domains of emotional intelligence and why they matter for performance
  • Build self-awareness by naming emotions, noticing triggers and comparing your view with others' feedback
  • Use a pause, name and choose routine to manage reactions under pressure
  • Recognize signs of stress in colleagues and respond with empathy and proper referral
  • Manage relationships: difficult conversations, repairing trust and influencing without force
