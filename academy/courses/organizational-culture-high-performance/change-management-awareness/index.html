Change Management Awareness

Module 04 / 6 · 50 min

Understand how people experience change, why they resist it and how to judge and build a team's readiness, so that new systems and ways of working actually stick.

In this module

  1. 1.Change is now part of the job
  2. 2.How people experience change
  3. 3.Understanding and overcoming resistance
  4. 4.Change readiness with ADKAR
  5. 5.Adapting to change yourself
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain why Ethiopian organizations face frequent change and what it means for employees
  • Describe the stages people move through during change and how to support each
  • Recognize the causes of resistance and respond with listening and involvement rather than pressure
  • Use the ADKAR model to judge change readiness and find the barrier point
  • Build personal adaptability habits
