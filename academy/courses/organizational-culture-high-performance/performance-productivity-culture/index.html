Performance and Productivity Culture

Module 03 / 6 · 60 min

Set clear goals, manage time and meetings well, and use small measured experiments so the team improves a little every week.

In this module

  1. 1.What a performance culture looks like
  2. 2.Goals that people can achieve
  3. 3.Time management: protect time for what matters
  4. 4.Meetings that earn their cost
  5. 5.Continuous improvement: small steps every week
  6. 6.Did it work? Judging the result with rates
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the habits of a performance culture: clear goals, measurement, feedback and recognition
  • Write SMART goals that connect a person's work to team and company results
  • Use a time audit and the urgent and important matrix to protect time for priority work
  • Calculate the cost of meetings and redesign them
  • Run a plan, do, check, act improvement experiment and judge the result with rates, not counts
