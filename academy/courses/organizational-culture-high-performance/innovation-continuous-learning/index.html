Innovation and Continuous Learning

Module 05 / 6 · 55 min

Build a learning mindset, use simple creativity tools to generate ideas, test them cheaply and share knowledge so the team does not depend on one person.

In this module

  1. 1.A learning mindset
  2. 2.Learning habits that keep skills current
  3. 3.Creativity at work
  4. 4.From idea to small test
  5. 5.Knowledge sharing: do not depend on one person
  6. 6.Learning from experience: the after-action review
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe a learning mindset and the habits that keep skills current
  • Use brainstorming rules and simple creativity prompts to generate ideas at work
  • Move an idea from suggestion to a small, measured test
  • Reduce key-person risk through knowledge sharing and documented know-how
  • Run an after-action review to turn experience into team learning

  • ISO 45003:2021 Psychological health and safety at work (good practice guidance)
  • Published good practice: Edmondson on psychological safety, the Thomas-Kilmann conflict modes, Prosci's ADKAR model, Dweck on mindset and Goleman on emotional intelligence
