Cross-border structuring and the syndication capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Bring in DFIs, export credit agencies and political risk cover, manage FX and sovereign risk, and negotiate the Ankober deal to signing.

In this module

  1. 1.DFIs and A/B loans
  2. 2.Export credit agency cover and MIGA guarantees
  3. 3.External loans and the FX rules
  4. 4.Currency mismatch: stressing the USD tranche
  5. 5.Sovereign risk after the debt restructuring
  6. 6.Capstone: the Ankober syndication simulation
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain A/B loans, export credit agency cover and MIGA guarantees and when each fits
  • Apply the external loan rules of FXD/01/2024 as amended by FXD/04/2026
  • Stress a mixed-currency structure for currency depreciation and design mitigants
  • Assess sovereign and transfer risk after Ethiopia's debt restructuring and negotiate a cross-border deal to signing

  • Erudite Africa course specification 1.o: Syndicated Loans and Cross-Border Project Finance, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)

  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/87/2024, SBB/88/2024, SBB/90/2024, SBB/95/2025, SBB/96/2025, NBE/INT/13/2026, FXD/01/2024 and FXD/04/2026
  • A commercial bank's Revised Credit Procedure Manual (August 2025), unnamed
  • Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025; Public Private Partnership Proclamation No. 1076/2018 as amended; Ministry of Finance Public Sector Debt Statistical Bulletin No. 56
