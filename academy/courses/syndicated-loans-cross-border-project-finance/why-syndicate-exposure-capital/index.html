Why banks syndicate: exposure limits, capital and the participation decision

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Work out how much of a large Ethiopian deal one bank can and should hold, and why the rest must be shared.

In this module

  1. 1.The 25% wall: why one bank cannot do it alone
  2. 2.Connected counterparties: finding the real group
  3. 3.The capital a ticket consumes
  4. 4.Club deals in the Ethiopian market
  5. 5.Who does what: arranger, agent, security agent, participant
  6. 6.Running case: should Bank D join, and for how much?
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the SBB/87/2024 large-exposure rules, including connected counterparties, to a proposed ticket
  • Measure the capital a syndicated share consumes under SBB/95/2025
  • Explain the roles of arranger, agent, security agent and participant, and what each one owes the others
  • Decide whether to join a syndication, and at what amount, from an information memorandum and your own headroom
