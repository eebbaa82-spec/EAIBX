Arranging the deal: mandate, due diligence, book-building and fees

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Win and run a syndication mandate from the pitch to the final allocation, with diligence, pricing and fees that will hold up.

In this module

  1. 1.Pitching for the mandate
  2. 2.Underwritten, best efforts or club
  3. 3.The information memorandum and due diligence
  4. 4.Book-building, market flex and allocation
  5. 5.Pricing and fees
  6. 6.From commitment to signing
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare underwritten, best efforts and club strategies and choose one for an Ethiopian deal
  • Plan the information memorandum and the due diligence a DFI participant will demand
  • Build the book, use market flex and allocate commitments fairly
  • Calculate arrangement, participation, commitment and agency fees and the all-in return to a participant
