Early warning signals and loan follow-up

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Catch deterioration in the first month it shows, through physical, financial and legal follow-up and a disciplined watch list.

In this module

  1. 1.Why follow up: good history is no guarantee
  2. 2.Reading early warning signals
  3. 3.Physical, financial and legal follow-up
  4. 4.Following up a project loan
  5. 5.Building a watch list
  6. 6.Escalation and the Bole case nine months on
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain why follow-up continues for the whole life of a loan
  • Recognize early warning signals from the account, the financials, management and the environment
  • Run physical, financial and legal follow-up, including project follow-up
  • Build a watch list with actions, owners and deadlines
  • Apply the escalation steps from first call to committee decision

  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/87/2024, SBB/88/2024, SBB/89/2024, SBB/90/2024 and SBB/95/2025
  • Ethiopian laws: Proclamations No. 97/1998 and 98/1998, Movable Property Security Right Proclamation No. 1147/2019, Financial Reporting Proclamation No. 847/2014
