Governance, ethics, exceptions and the integrated capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 65 min

Know where judgment ends and authority begins, handle conflicts and insider lending correctly, and lead a committee through a hard case.

In this module you will learn to

  • Distinguish an exception from a deviation and route each to the right authority
  • Apply related party and insider lending rules on limits and arm's-length terms
  • Handle conflicts of interest and confidentiality in committee
  • Explain the rules for syndicated loans and environmental assessment
  • Prepare and present an integrated committee paper on a distressed group
