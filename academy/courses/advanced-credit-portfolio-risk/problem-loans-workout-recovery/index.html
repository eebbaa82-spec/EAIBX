Problem loans: classification, provisioning, workout and recovery

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Classify and provision honestly, then choose between rescue and enforcement by comparing what each option will really recover.

In this module

  1. 1.Classifying loans
  2. 2.Computing provisions
  3. 3.Non-accrual and IFRS 9
  4. 4.When a loan goes to workout, and the toolkit
  5. 5.Negotiating a workout
  6. 6.Rescue or foreclose? Compare present values
  7. 7.Legal recovery, write-off and post write-off follow-up
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Classify loans under NBE Directive SBB/90/2024 and explain non-accrual
  • Compute minimum provisions after eligible collateral deductions
  • Decide when a loan moves to workout and which tools fit the case
  • Compare restructuring and foreclosure by present value of recoveries
  • Explain foreclosure, write-off and post write-off follow-up
