← Customer Service and Retail Banking Excellence

Communication skills: listening, questioning and explaining costs

Module 03 / 5 · 60 min

Listen actively, ask the right questions and explain fees, rates and the annual percentage rate clearly to customers of every language and literacy level.

In this module

  1. 1.Active listening
  2. 2.Asking the right questions
  3. 3.Explaining fees, rates and the APR
  4. 4.Flat and declining-balance interest
  5. 5.Different languages and literacy levels
  6. 6.Explaining a loan rejection
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Use active listening and open and closed questions to understand a customer's real need
  • Explain fees, interest and the annual percentage rate (APR) in plain words and numbers
  • Compare flat and declining-balance interest so customers understand the true cost
  • Adapt explanations for customers with low literacy or a different language, and explain a loan decline respectfully

  • National Bank of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1359/2025; Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 (as reported)
