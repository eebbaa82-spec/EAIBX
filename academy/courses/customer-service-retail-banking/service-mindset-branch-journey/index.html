The service mindset and the branch customer journey

Module 01 / 5 · 55 min · Free preview

Understand why service decides who wins in a crowded Ethiopian banking market, and walk the customer's path through a busy branch from the door to the goodbye.

In this module

  1. 1.Why service matters in a competitive market
  2. 2.Meet the running case: Bank A's Kera branch
  3. 3.Ethiopian service culture, respect and first impressions
  4. 4.The customer journey through a branch
  5. 5.Queues: why they grow and what front-line staff can do
  6. 6.From mindset to a service promise
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain why service quality matters more as banks, mobile money and new entrants compete for the same customers
  • Apply Ethiopian norms of respect, greeting and language to first impressions at the branch
  • Map the customer journey through a branch and name the moments of truth at each stage
  • Estimate how teller capacity and service time drive queues, and suggest practical fixes
