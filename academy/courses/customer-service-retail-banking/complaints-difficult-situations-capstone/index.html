Complaints, difficult situations and the ten branch moments

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Handle complaints through a clear process with timelines, escalation and records, calm angry customers, and bring every skill together in the ten branch moment role plays scored with a service rubric.

In this module

  1. 1.Why complaints matter
  2. 2.The complaint process: receive, record, resolve, respond
  3. 3.Calming angry customers
  4. 4.Common branch problems: failed transfers and system outages
  5. 5.Capstone: the ten branch moments
  6. 6.The service rubric and the assessment
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Follow a complaint process from receipt to resolution, with timelines, escalation and records
  • Calm an angry customer using a structured approach
  • Handle common branch problems such as failed transfers and captured cards
  • Perform the ten branch moment role plays and score them with a service rubric
