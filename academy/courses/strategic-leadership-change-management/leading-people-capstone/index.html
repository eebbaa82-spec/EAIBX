Leading people, decisions and the capstone roadmap

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

In this module you will learn to

  • Coach team members with the GROW model and use emotional intelligence in difficult moments
  • Build inclusive, high-performing teams, including women and staff of different generations
  • Recognize decision biases, set decision rights and lead ethically under pressure
  • Build and present a 12-month change roadmap and plan a small real change in your own unit
