Leading major change

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Use Kotter, ADKAR, change governance, stakeholder mapping, impact assessment and communication plans to lead mergers and system migrations.

In this module

  1. 1.Why change fails, and Kotter's eight steps
  2. 2.ADKAR: change one person at a time
  3. 3.Change governance: who decides what
  4. 4.Stakeholder mapping and engagement
  5. 5.Change impact assessment
  6. 6.Communication plans for big change
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain why change programs fail and use Kotter's eight steps and ADKAR to plan and diagnose
  • Set up change governance with clear decision rights
  • Map stakeholders by power and interest and plan how to engage each group
  • Assess change impact and build a communication plan for a merger or system migration
