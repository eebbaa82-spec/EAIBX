Inventory and Asset Management

Module 05 / 6 · 60 min

Keep stock records that match the store, value stock correctly, run and investigate stock counts, set reorder levels, and keep a fixed asset register that supports depreciation, insurance and tax.

In this module

  1. 1.Stock records: from purchase order to issue note
  2. 2.Valuing stock: weighted average and FIFO
  3. 3.The stock count and investigating gaps
  4. 4.Inventory controls: reorder levels, ABC and segregation
  5. 5.The fixed asset register
  6. 6.Book depreciation, tax depreciation and asset control
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Keep stock records from purchase order to issue note and reconcile them to the ledger
  • Value stock with weighted average or FIFO and explain the effect on profit
  • Plan a stock count, calculate the gap and investigate it properly
  • Set reorder levels and inventory controls such as segregation of duties and ABC analysis
  • Maintain a fixed asset register and apply book and tax depreciation rules
