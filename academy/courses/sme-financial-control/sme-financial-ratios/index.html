SME Financial Ratios

Module 06 / 6 · 60 min

Calculate and interpret liquidity, profitability, efficiency and leverage ratios from one set of statements, and turn them into decisions and a story a bank will trust.

In this module

  1. 1.Reading ratios the right way
  2. 2.Liquidity ratios
  3. 3.Profitability ratios
  4. 4.Efficiency ratios
  5. 5.Leverage, coverage and the lender's view
  6. 6.From ratios to decisions: DuPont and the monthly review
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Calculate liquidity ratios and explain why the current ratio alone can mislead
  • Calculate gross, operating and net margins, return on assets and return on equity
  • Calculate DSO, DIO, DPO, the cash conversion cycle and asset turnover
  • Read leverage and debt service ratios the way a lender does
  • Link ratios through DuPont analysis and turn them into actions
