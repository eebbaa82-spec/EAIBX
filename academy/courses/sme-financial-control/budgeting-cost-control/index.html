Budgeting and Cost Control

Module 04 / 6 · 65 min

Build an operational budget from volumes and prices, classify costs, find break-even, explain variances by price and volume, and control costs without hurting the business.

In this module

  1. 1.Building the operational budget
  2. 2.Classifying costs
  3. 3.Contribution margin and break-even
  4. 4.Budget vs actual and variance analysis
  5. 5.Flexed budgets: price and volume
  6. 6.Cost control that works
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build a monthly operational budget from sales volumes, prices and cost drivers, including tax
  • Classify costs as fixed or variable, direct or indirect, and controllable or not
  • Calculate contribution margin, break-even revenue and margin of safety
  • Prepare a budget vs actual report and split variances into price and volume effects
  • Choose cost control actions that respect the Labour Proclamation and protect quality
