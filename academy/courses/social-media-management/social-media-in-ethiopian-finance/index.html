Social media in Ethiopian finance

Module 01 / 5 · 45 min · Free preview

See why Ethiopian banks and microfinance institutions run official pages, which platforms customers use, what they expect, and the rules that frame every post.

  1. 1.Why a bank runs official pages

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the four jobs an official social media page does for a financial institution
  • Describe the role of the main platforms Ethiopian customers use, including Telegram
  • List what customers expect from a bank page and how good and bad pages differ
  • Name the Ethiopian laws and NBE rules that apply to a bank's social media
