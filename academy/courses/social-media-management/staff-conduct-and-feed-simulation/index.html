Staff conduct online and the feed simulation

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Follow the staff social media policy on personal accounts, confidentiality and political or ethnic content, then apply the whole course in the 15-post feed simulation.

In this module

  1. 1.Your personal account and the bank
  2. 2.Confidentiality and inside information
  3. 3.Political, ethnic content and the hate speech law
  4. 4.When you see something wrong
  5. 5.The capstone: a feed of 15 posts
  6. 6.Rehearsal: three posts from the feed
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how personal accounts affect the institution and what the staff social media policy requires
  • Avoid disclosing confidential, customer or inside information online
  • Apply the hate speech and disinformation law to political and ethnic content
  • Report a colleague's or a page's harmful post through the right channel
  • Respond to a simulated feed of 15 posts with accuracy, the right tone and full compliance
