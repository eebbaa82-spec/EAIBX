Community management and complaints

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Answer comments and messages on time and in the right tone, move private matters to secure channels, moderate by clear rules, escalate correctly and log every complaint.

In this module

  1. 1.Response time standards
  2. 2.Tone that calms: the SAFE reply
  3. 3.Moving to private channels, never sharing account data
  4. 4.Moderation rules: what stays, what goes
  5. 5.Escalation: getting the right team involved
  6. 6.Recording social media complaints
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply response time standards and measure the page against them
  • Reply in a calm, respectful tone using the SAFE method
  • Move account matters to secure channels without ever sharing or asking for account data in public
  • Moderate comments using written rules and escalate issues to the right team
  • Record social media complaints in the complaint register and track them to resolution
