Running the pages day to day

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Plan a content calendar, run posts through approval and scheduling, write in the bank's voice and languages, and keep product, investment and customer photo posts within the rules.

In this module

  1. 1.Content pillars and the monthly calendar
  2. 2.From draft to scheduled post
  3. 3.Brand voice and tone
  4. 4.Languages and accessibility
  5. 5.Product posts and investment promotion
  6. 6.Customer stories, photos and consent
  7. 7.Measuring a post
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build a monthly content calendar balanced across content pillars
  • Run every post through drafting, fact checking, compliance review, approval and scheduling
  • Write in a consistent brand voice, in local languages, and make posts accessible
  • Apply advertising, capital market and data protection rules to product posts and customer photos
  • Measure a post with reach, engagement rate and click-through
