Serving women customers

Module 04 / 5 · 50 min

Make every channel work for women: respectful communication, a welcoming branch, flexible hours, women agents, safe mobile banking and literacy-friendly materials.

In this module

  1. 1.Communication that respects and informs
  2. 2.A branch that welcomes women
  3. 3.Time: flexible hours and appointments
  4. 4.Women agents close to home
  5. 5.Mobile channels used safely
  6. 6.Literacy-friendly materials and contracts
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Communicate respectfully and clearly with women customers of different backgrounds
  • Describe branch, hours and agent arrangements that remove time and mobility barriers
  • Help a woman customer use mobile channels safely, including fraud awareness
  • Explain products and contracts to customers with limited literacy using a check-back method

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 4.f Gender-Sensitive Financial Services, Level 1 (October 2026)
  • World Bank Africa Gender Innovation Lab with the NBE: Making Ethiopia's Financial Sector Work for Women (March 2024)
  • National Financial Inclusion Strategy II (2021 to 2025), the NFIS-III refresh and the Women's Financial Inclusion Scorecard (NBE with the World Bank)
  • NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025 and NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/91/2024, SBB/72/2019, NBE/INT/13/2026, Instruction FCPED/142/26; consumer protection rules reported as FCP/01/2020
  • A commercial bank's Revised Credit Procedure Manual (August 2025), unnamed, including its references to Proclamation No. 1147/2019 and Directive MCR/01/2020
