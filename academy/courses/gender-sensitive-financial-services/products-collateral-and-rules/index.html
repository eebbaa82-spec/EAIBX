Products, collateral alternatives and the rules

Module 05 / 5 · 65 min

Know the products that fit women, from savings and group loans to cash-flow SME loans, movable collateral and interest-free options, apply the rules on consent and non-discrimination, and redesign the Adama café loan.

In this module

  1. 1.Women's savings accounts
  2. 2.Group lending
  3. 3.Collateral-light SME loans: cash flow first
  4. 4.Movable collateral and the registry
  5. 5.Interest-free options
  6. 6.The rules: consent, fairness and equality
  7. 7.Capstone: redesigning the Adama café loan
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain women's savings accounts, group lending, collateral-light SME loans and interest-free options
  • Use cash-flow analysis and DSCR to judge a small business loan with thin records
  • Describe movable collateral and the Movable Collateral Registry as alternatives to land and buildings
  • Apply the rules on spousal consent, non-discrimination, board diversity and workplace equality
  • Redesign the Adama café appraisal using cash flow and movable collateral
