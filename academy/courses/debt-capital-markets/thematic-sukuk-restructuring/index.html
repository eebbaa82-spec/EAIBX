Green bonds, sukuk, sovereign restructuring and the capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Align a green bond with the ICMA principles, design sukuk for interest-free investors, understand debt sustainability and the Common Framework, and present Bank A's final term sheet.

In this module

  1. 1.The Green, Social and Sustainability Bond Principles
  2. 2.External review and impact reporting
  3. 3.Sukuk for interest-free investors
  4. 4.Debt sustainability and the Common Framework
  5. 5.Bondholder negotiations and lessons for corporates
  6. 6.Capstone: Bank A's green bond term sheet
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the four core components of the ICMA Green Bond Principles and the Social and Sustainability Bond guidance to an Ethiopian issue
  • Design ijara, murabaha and mudarabah sukuk structures for interest-free investors
  • Explain debt sustainability analysis, the G20 Common Framework and bondholder negotiations, and their implications for Ethiopian corporates
  • Present a complete term sheet and pricing rationale for a five-year ETB 2 billion green bond
