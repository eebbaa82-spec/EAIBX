Price, yield, the yield curve and the Eurobond story

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Price T-bills and coupon bonds, measure interest rate risk with duration, build a Birr yield curve from auction data and read Ethiopia's 2014 Eurobond as a case in pricing and default.

In this module

  1. 1.Pricing a T-bill
  2. 2.Pricing a coupon bond
  3. 3.Duration: how much the price moves
  4. 4.Building a Birr yield curve
  5. 5.Case: Ethiopia's 2014 Eurobond
  6. 6.What the Eurobond means for future issuers
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Price a T-bill from its yield and convert between discount and investment yields
  • Price a coupon bond, compute yield to maturity, current yield, accrued interest and modified duration
  • Build and interpret a Birr yield curve from T-bill auction results and extend it to a five-year benchmark
  • Explain what Ethiopia's 2014 Eurobond was, why the December 2023 coupon was missed and what that meant for prices
