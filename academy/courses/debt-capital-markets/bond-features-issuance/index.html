← Debt Capital Markets (DCM)

Bond features, documentation and the issuance process

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Read and negotiate the key terms of a bond offering and run an Ethiopian issue from mandate through ECMA registration, marketing, settlement and ESX listing.

In this module

  1. 1.The anatomy of a bond
  2. 2.Security, ranking and subordination
  3. 3.Covenants and the bondholder representative
  4. 4.From mandate to listing
  5. 5.ECMA registration, fees and offer types
  6. 6.Designing for Ethiopian investors
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain coupon, maturity, amortization, call and put options, security, covenants and the bondholder representative, and how each shifts risk
  • Sequence the issuance process from mandate to listing and name the documents and approvals at each step
  • Apply ECMA's registration fee for a bond offer and distinguish a public offer from a private placement
  • Judge which terms to offer investors in a market with few precedents

  • Ministry of Finance, Public Sector Debt Statistical Bulletin No. 56 (February 2026)
  • ICMA Green Bond Principles, Social Bond Principles and Sustainability Bond Guidelines; G20 Common Framework for Debt Treatments
  • Commercial Code of 2021 (reported as Proclamation No. 1243/2021)
