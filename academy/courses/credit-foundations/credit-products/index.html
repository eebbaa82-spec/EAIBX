Credit products

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Match the purpose and cash cycle of a need to the product built for it, because a wrong product is the first step toward a problem loan.

In this module

  1. 1.Short money for short needs
  2. 2.Term loans by tenure and grace
  3. 3.The overdraft facility
  4. 4.Reading an overdraft statement: turnover and swing
  5. 5.Overdrawals and merchandise loans
  6. 6.Trade products, guarantees and consumer loans
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the main product families and their key terms
  • Match loan tenor to the life of the asset or the length of the cash cycle
  • Read an overdraft statement for turnover and swing
  • Choose between overdraft, overdrawal, merchandise loan, L/C facility, pre-shipment advance and guarantee
