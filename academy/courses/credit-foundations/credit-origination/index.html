Credit origination

Module 03 / 5 · 60 min

Most bad loans are visible at the front door: practise the eligibility check, interview, documents, credit report and business visit that catch them.

In this module

  1. 1.Eligibility: the first filter
  2. 2.The intake interview
  3. 3.Documents and financial statements
  4. 4.Credit information from the NBE Credit Reference Bureau
  5. 5.The business visit
  6. 6.Checking documents and authority
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the general eligibility criteria to an applicant
  • Run an interview with open and probing questions and record red flags
  • Identify the financial statements and documents each type of applicant must present
  • Obtain and use a credit report correctly and confidentially
  • Conduct a business visit and write up variations in a due diligence report

  • Ethiopian laws as cited: Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025, Movable Property Security Right Proclamation No. 1147/2019, Proclamation No. 97/1998
