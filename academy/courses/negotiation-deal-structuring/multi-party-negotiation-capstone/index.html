Multi-party negotiation and the hospital group capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Lead a negotiation among a PE fund, a DFI, a founding family and a local bank over a growth investment and refinancing for an Ethiopian private hospital group, and produce and critique a term sheet.

In this module

  1. 1.Mapping a multi-party deal: Hiwot Specialty Hospitals
  2. 2.Coalitions, sequencing and the single negotiating text
  3. 3.Lenders and DFIs: refinancing, ranking and exposure limits
  4. 4.Ownership and governance among three shareholder groups
  5. 5.Regulators, approvals and the closing plan
  6. 6.Capstone: the term sheet and the term sheet critique
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Map the parties, interests and BATNAs in a multi-party deal and build coalitions
  • Structure equity, DFI terms and a bank refinancing so the sources and uses balance and exposure rules are met
  • Negotiate governance among three shareholder groups and handle regulators and approvals
  • Draft a term sheet for the capstone simulation and critique a term sheet from a real deal
