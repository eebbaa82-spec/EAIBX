← Negotiation and Deal Structuring

Governance, control, exit and dispute resolution

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Negotiate board seats, reserved matters, information rights, transfer rights and deadlock mechanisms, plan exits through the ESX, trade sales and options, and choose an enforceable dispute resolution route.

In this module

  1. 1.Board seats, reserved matters and information rights
  2. 2.Pre-emption, drag-along and tag-along
  3. 3.Deadlock and how to break it
  4. 4.Exit planning: ESX listing, trade sale and options
  5. 5.Enforceability of shareholder rights in Ethiopia
  6. 6.Dispute resolution: arbitration seat, courts and enforcement
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Negotiate board composition, reserved matters, vetoes and information rights for a minority investor
  • Design drag, tag, pre-emption and deadlock provisions and compute their effects
  • Compare exit routes in Ethiopia, including an ESX listing, a trade sale and put and call options
  • Assess the enforceability of shareholder rights and options in Ethiopia
  • Choose an arbitration seat and enforcement route for a cross-border deal
