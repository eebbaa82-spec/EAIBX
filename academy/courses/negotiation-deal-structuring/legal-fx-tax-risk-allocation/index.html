← Negotiation and Deal Structuring

Ethiopian legal, FX and tax constraints, and risk allocation

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Work within Ethiopian company law, foreign ownership rules, FX and repatriation rules and tax on transfers, and allocate risk through warranties, indemnities, MAC clauses and conditions.

In this module

  1. 1.Share company or PLC, articles and shareholder agreements
  2. 2.Foreign ownership limits and entry requirements
  3. 3.FX rules: bringing money in, borrowing abroad and getting money out
  4. 4.Tax on transfers, dividends and related-party payments
  5. 5.Warranties, indemnities and MAC clauses
  6. 6.Sharing FX and regulatory risk from signing to closing
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how the choice between a share company and a PLC affects structuring
  • Check foreign ownership access and minimum capital before structuring a deal
  • Apply FX rules on external loans and repatriation of dividends and proceeds
  • Identify the tax points on share transfers, dividends, indirect transfers and related-party payments
  • Draft and negotiate warranties, indemnities, MAC clauses and FX and regulatory risk sharing
