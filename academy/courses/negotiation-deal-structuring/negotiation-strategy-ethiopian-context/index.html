Negotiation strategy in the Ethiopian deal context

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Prepare a deal negotiation from interests, BATNA, reservation price and ZOPA, use anchors and concessions with discipline, and adapt to family owners, state counterparts and the role of trust in Ethiopia.

In this module

  1. 1.Interests, positions and the issues on the table
  2. 2.BATNA, reservation price and the ZOPA
  3. 3.Anchoring, concessions and package offers
  4. 4.Negotiating in the Ethiopian context
  5. 5.State counterparts, regulators and sequencing
  6. 6.The negotiation plan and the practice negotiation
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Separate interests from positions and map them for every party to a deal
  • Estimate each side's BATNA and reservation price and compute the ZOPA
  • Plan anchors, concessions and package offers, and respond to an extreme anchor
  • Adapt the process to Ethiopian family owners, state counterparts, hierarchy and intermediaries
  • Write a negotiation plan and run a practice negotiation against a rubric
