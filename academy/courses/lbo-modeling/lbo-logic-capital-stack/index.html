LBO logic, targets and the capital stack

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Why sponsors buy companies with borrowed money, what makes a good target, how the layers of debt and equity are arranged, and which Ethiopian rules limit how much leverage a deal can carry.

In this module

  1. 1.What a leveraged buyout is
  2. 2.What makes a good LBO target
  3. 3.Three ways an LBO creates equity value
  4. 4.The capital stack: senior, mezzanine, vendor notes, equity
  5. 5.How Ethiopian bank rules cap acquisition debt
  6. 6.Company law, competition and bank equity limits
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how a leveraged buyout creates equity value through EBITDA growth, multiple expansion and deleveraging
  • Screen an Ethiopian company against the cash flow, asset and management tests of a good LBO target
  • Describe senior debt, mezzanine, vendor notes and equity, their ranking and typical terms
  • Apply the large exposure limit of SBB/87/2024 and the investment limits of SBB/92/2024 to an acquisition financing
