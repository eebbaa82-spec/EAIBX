Leveraged Buyout (LBO) Modeling

Build, stress and negotiate leveraged acquisitions in a market of high birr rates, bank exposure limits and USD debt risk

An advanced course that builds a full LBO model for a fictional Ethiopian dairy processor, then structures, stresses and bids for a family pharmaceutical distributor with local bank debt and DFI mezzanine.

Start the course

0 of 5 modules completed

What you will be able to do

  • Explain what an LBO is, why sponsors use leverage and how value is created
  • Build an LBO model with sources and uses, an operating model, a multi-tranche debt schedule, a cash sweep, exit and returns
  • Interpret IRR and money multiple and their sensitivity to price, leverage and exit
  • Adapt the LBO to Ethiopian conditions: high birr rates, bank exposure limits, offshore USD debt, FX risk and limited leverage
  • Use the model to stress covenants and to negotiate price and structure

The case you will follow

Sululta Dairy Processing PLC

Sululta Dairy Processing PLC is a fictional dairy processor north of Addis Ababa that collects milk from about 4,000 smallholders and earned EBITDA of ETB 300 million on revenue of ETB 2,000 million. Rift Valley Capital Partners, a fictional regional private equity fund, buys it from its retiring founder, Ato Kebede Woldemariam, at 6.0x EBITDA (ETB 1,800 million) with a ETB 700 million senior loan from a two-bank club and ETB 1,200 million of equity. Modules 1 to 3 build its model, from sources and uses to a 25.1% plan IRR. Modules 3 to 5 move to the capstone: a management buyout of Tena Pharma Distribution PLC, a family-owned medicines importer and distributor, led by CEO Dr. Rahel Tadesse and CFO Samuel Abebe with Rift Valley, financed by birr senior debt, USD DFI mezzanine and a family vendor note, which teams stress and bid for.

For
Investment banking and private equity analysts, associates and deal team members, and bank staff who structure or approve acquisition finance. Prerequisite: Financial Modeling and Valuation (3.a) Level 1 or equivalent spreadsheet modeling skills.

The 5 modules

  1. Module 01Free previewLBO logic, targets and the capital stackWhy sponsors buy companies with borrowed money, what makes a good target, how the layers of debt and equity are arranged, and which Ethiopian rules limit how much leverage a deal can carry.6 lessons · 60 min · quiz
  2. Module 02Building the model: sources and uses to cash sweepBuild Sululta Dairy's LBO model step by step: sources and uses, a nominal birr operating model, free cash flow, a debt schedule with a cash sweep, and the interest circularity that links them.6 lessons · 70 min · quiz
  3. Module 03Exit, returns and advanced debt structuresValue the exit, compute and attribute IRR and MOIC, test their sensitivity, then build the multi-tranche structure of the capstone deal with PIK mezzanine, a revolver, amortization profiles and covenant tests.6 lessons · 70 min · quiz
  4. Module 04Ethiopian structuring and lower-leverage buyoutsAdapt the LBO to Ethiopia: local bank appetite, offshore holding companies, USD debt against birr cash flows, tax and dividend repatriation, foreign investor eligibility, and the minority, family and privatization deals that suit the market.6 lessons · 65 min · quiz
  5. Module 05Stress tests, negotiation and the capstone bidStress the Tena Pharma structure for depreciation, rate and EBITDA shocks, analyze covenant breaches, find the maximum price, use vendor finance and earn-outs to bridge the gap, and write the investment memo that recommends a bid.6 lessons · 70 min · quiz
  6. Officer simulationA buyout deal cycle as acquisition finance associate at Bank A CapitalWork as acquisition finance associate: 11 real-life items, decisions with consequences and a hand-over note. Pass with 70% to qualify for the certificate.
  7. Final examFinal exam and certificate20 applied questions across all 5 modules. Pass with 70% to download your certificate.
