Stress tests, negotiation and the capstone bid

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Stress the Tena Pharma structure for depreciation, rate and EBITDA shocks, analyze covenant breaches, find the maximum price, use vendor finance and earn-outs to bridge the gap, and write the investment memo that recommends a bid.

In this module

  1. 1.Designing Ethiopian stress scenarios
  2. 2.Covenant breach analysis
  3. 3.Maximum price analysis
  4. 4.Vendor finance and earn-outs
  5. 5.The investment memo and structuring note
  6. 6.Capstone: recommending the Tena bid
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Design downside scenarios for depreciation, interest rates and EBITDA and read their effect on IRR and covenants
  • Diagnose a covenant breach and choose between cure, waiver, reset and restructuring
  • Compute the maximum price that meets a target IRR in the plan and downside cases
  • Structure vendor finance and earn-outs to bridge a price gap and protect returns
  • Write an investment memo and structuring note that recommend a bid
