Needs discovery

Module 03 / 5 · 55 min

Use SPIN questions and active listening to uncover and size the customer's real pain points before presenting anything.

In this module

  1. 1.Why questions beat pitches
  2. 2.SPIN questioning
  3. 3.Active listening and indirect signals
  4. 4.Finding and sizing pain points
  5. 5.Exporter needs and the FX rules
  6. 6.Confirming needs before you present
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Ask situation, problem, implication and need-payoff questions in a natural sequence
  • Listen actively, paraphrase and read indirect signals common in Ethiopian business conversations
  • Turn a stated problem into a sized pain point with Birr figures
  • Summarize and prioritize needs and get the customer's agreement before presenting
