Negotiation basics and the capstone

Module 05 / 5 · 65 min

Negotiate on interests rather than positions, know your BATNA and walk-away point, trade concessions, respect Ethiopian business norms and run the capstone role-plays.

In this module

  1. 1.Positions versus interests
  2. 2.BATNA, reservation point and ZOPA
  3. 3.Preparing the Tesfa Hiwot negotiation
  4. 4.Making and trading concessions
  5. 5.Cultural norms in Ethiopian business negotiation
  6. 6.When the other side is connected to the bank
  7. 7.Capstone: the role-plays and the rubric
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Distinguish positions from interests and find options that serve both sides
  • Work out each side's BATNA, reservation point and the zone of possible agreement
  • Plan and trade concessions within the bank's pricing authority and related party rules
  • Adapt negotiation style to Ethiopian business norms
  • Prepare and perform the capstone sales call and payroll negotiation against the rubric
