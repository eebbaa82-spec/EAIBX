Presenting, objections and closing

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Present a tailored proposal in benefits, handle price objections with total cost and fair comparisons, close without pressure and follow up so the relationship grows.

In this module

  1. 1.Features, advantages, benefits
  2. 2.Building Kaffa Ridge's proposal
  3. 3.Handling price objections
  4. 4.Comparing with competitors fairly
  5. 5.Closing techniques
  6. 6.Documentation, after-sales follow-up and CRM
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Translate product features into benefits tied to a confirmed customer need
  • Handle price and other objections using listen, acknowledge, explore and respond
  • Compare Bank A's offer with a competitor's fairly and accurately
  • Choose a closing technique that fits the customer and record the deal and follow-up in the CRM
