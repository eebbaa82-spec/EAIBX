Prospecting and preparation

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Build and rank a target list, use referrals and community networks respectfully, research a prospect and plan a call that earns the next meeting.

  2. 2.Referrals and the value chain

In this module you will learn to

  • Build a target list from existing customers, value chains and local business activity, and score it
  • Use referrals and community networks such as iddir, equb, faith and business associations ethically
  • Research a prospect's business cycle before the first call
  • Write a call plan with an objective, questions, likely objections and a next step
