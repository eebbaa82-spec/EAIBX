Selling in a bank

Module 01 / 5 · 50 min · Free preview

Understand why a bank sells by advising, where consumer protection draws the line, and how the sales cycle runs from first contact to follow-up.

In this module

  1. 1.Selling versus advising
  2. 2.Why selling skills matter more now
  3. 3.Ethics and consumer protection limits
  4. 4.The bank sales cycle
  5. 5.Meet the running cases
  6. 6.Targets, incentives and conduct
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the difference between pushing a product and advising a customer
  • Apply the fair treatment, disclosure and suitability principles of consumer protection to everyday selling
  • Describe the seven stages of the bank sales cycle and measure conversion between them
  • Balance sales targets with conduct indicators such as dormancy and complaints
