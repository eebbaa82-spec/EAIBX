← Ethiopian Banking Law and Regulation

Prudential rules in plain language

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Understand what the NBE's capital, liquidity, concentration, investment and provisioning rules require and why each one protects depositors.

In this module

  1. 1.Capital adequacy: SBB/95/2025
  2. 2.Liquidity: cash to meet withdrawals
  3. 3.Large exposures: SBB/87/2024
  4. 4.Related parties: SBB/88/2024
  5. 5.Investment limits: SBB/92/2024
  6. 6.Loan classification and provisioning: SBB/90/2024
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the three capital ratios of SBB/95/2025 and compute a simple capital ratio
  • Describe why liquidity rules and the loan-to-deposit ratio matter
  • Apply the large exposure and related-party limits to simple figures
  • State the SBB/92/2024 investment limits
  • Classify a loan by days past due and compute minimum provisions under SBB/90/2024

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
