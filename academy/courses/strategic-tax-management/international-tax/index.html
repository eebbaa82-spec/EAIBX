International Tax Considerations

Module 04 / 6 · 65 min

Handle payments abroad, permanent establishment, tax treaties and transfer pricing so cross-border dealings are taxed once and correctly.

In this module

  1. 1.Payments abroad and imported services
  2. 2.Permanent establishment: the 91-day rule
  3. 3.Double taxation and tax treaties
  4. 4.Transfer pricing fundamentals
  5. 5.Documentation and benchmarking
  6. 6.Offshore transfers and repatriation
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the withholding and VAT rules to payments to non-residents and to imported services
  • Recognize when a foreign contractor creates a permanent establishment under the 91-day rule
  • Use tax treaties correctly and know when to confirm their status
  • Apply Transfer Pricing Directive No. 981/2024 and prepare a contemporaneous file
  • Explain the offshore indirect transfer rule and the tax on repatriated profit

  • Federal Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016 as amended by No. 1434/2026; Penalty Waiver Directive No. 189/2025; VAT Proclamation No. 1341/2024; Electronic Invoicing System Administration Directive No. 1142/2026

  • Practitioner summaries of the 2025 and 2026 changes (PwC, KPMG, EY, Grant Thornton Ethiopia, 5A Law Firm, Ethio Alliance) where the gazetted text could not be read, as noted in the lessons

Examples and companies are fictional. Tax rates and rules are shown as they stood when the course was written; always check the current proclamations, directives and guidance from the Ministry of Revenues before filing, and take professional advice for your own case.
