Tax Due Diligence

Module 02 / 6 · 65 min

Check your own company with a tax health check, rate tax risks in a register and examine an acquisition target before you sign.

In this module

  1. 1.Health check, due diligence and compliance review
  2. 2.Running a tax health check
  3. 3.Reconcile before you rely
  4. 4.Rating tax risk: likelihood, amount and evidence
  5. 5.Compliance review: processes, controls and records
  6. 6.From findings to protection
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Distinguish a tax health check, an acquisition tax due diligence and a compliance review
  • Run a tax-by-tax health check with reconciliations between accounts and returns
  • Rate tax risks by likelihood, amount and evidence and build a risk register
  • Turn due diligence findings into price adjustments, indemnities, escrows and conditions
