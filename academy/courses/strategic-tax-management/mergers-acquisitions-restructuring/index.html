Mergers, Acquisitions and Restructuring

Module 05 / 6 · 65 min

Choose between share and asset deals, price the tax on asset transfers and plan reorganizations that qualify for relief.

In this module

  1. 1.Share deal or asset deal
  2. 2.Tax on asset transfers
  3. 3.Share acquisitions: sellers' gains and inherited history
  4. 4.Reorganization relief
  5. 5.Restructuring a group
  6. 6.After the deal: integration and compliance
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare share and asset acquisitions from the buyer's and the seller's side
  • Compute the main tax costs of an asset transfer and a share sale, including capital gains and stamp duty
  • Apply the change-in-control rule for losses and the reorganization relief test
  • Plan post-deal integration so the combined group stays compliant
