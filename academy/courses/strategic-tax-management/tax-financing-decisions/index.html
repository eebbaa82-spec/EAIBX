Tax Implications in Financing Decisions

Module 03 / 6 · 65 min

Compare debt, equity, leasing and buying on an after-tax basis, within the interest limits, the dividend rules and the MAT floor.

In this module

  1. 1.Debt or equity: the interest tax shield
  2. 2.Limits on interest deductions
  3. 3.Equity, dividends and retained profit
  4. 4.How leasing works in Ethiopia
  5. 5.Lease or buy: an after-tax comparison
  6. 6.Tax in capital investment decisions
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute the interest tax shield and the after-tax cost of debt, and test it against MAT
  • Apply the limits on interest deductions, including the rate cap and the share-acquisition rule
  • Explain how dividends, retained profit and capital increases are taxed
  • Compare buying, hire purchase and operating leases under the leasing proclamation and the tax rules
  • Include depreciation, the 50% capital deduction and loss rules in capital investment decisions
