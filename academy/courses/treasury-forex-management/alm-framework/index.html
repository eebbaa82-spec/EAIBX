The ALM framework: repricing gap, earnings and economic value

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Measure how a change in the National Bank Rate flows through a bank's earnings and economic value, and read the result the way an ALCO must.

In this module

  1. 1.What ALM manages and who decides
  2. 2.The rate environment: National Bank Rate, corridor and market rates
  3. 3.Building the repricing gap
  4. 4.Earnings at risk
  5. 5.Economic value of equity and duration
  6. 6.Inflation, real rates and the end of the credit growth cap
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what asset and liability management covers and how the ALCO uses it
  • Describe the interest-rate-based monetary framework, the National Bank Rate and its corridor
  • Build a repricing gap and compute earnings at risk for a rate shock
  • Estimate the change in economic value of equity from duration
  • Assess how high inflation and the end of the credit growth cap change ALM decisions
