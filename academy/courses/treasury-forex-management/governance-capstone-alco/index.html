Market risk governance and the capstone ALCO

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Set and monitor treasury limits, report as an independent middle office, and write and defend Bank A's ALCO paper.

In this module

  1. 1.The limits framework
  2. 2.Middle office reporting and escalation
  3. 3.Model risk in ALM

In this module you will learn to

  • Design a treasury limits framework that links to board risk appetite
  • Prepare middle office reports and escalate breaches
  • Recognize model risk in ALM and VaR assumptions
  • Structure an ALCO paper with clear decisions
  • Recommend pricing, investment and funding actions for Bank A
