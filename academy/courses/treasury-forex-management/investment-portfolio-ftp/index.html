Investment portfolio and funds transfer pricing

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Price and classify the bank's securities within NBE limits, and use funds transfer pricing to price products and measure branch profitability.

In this module

  1. 1.What the investment portfolio is for
  2. 2.T-bill maths: price, discount and yield
  3. 3.Accounting classification under IFRS 9
  4. 4.Investment limits under SBB/92/2024
  5. 5.Matched-maturity funds transfer pricing
  6. 6.Using FTP for products and branch profitability
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the instruments in an Ethiopian bank's investment portfolio and their role
  • Price a T-bill and convert between discount and yield
  • Classify securities under IFRS 9 and explain the ALM consequences
  • Apply the SBB/92/2024 investment limits to a proposed investment
  • Build a matched-maturity FTP rate and use it for pricing and branch results
