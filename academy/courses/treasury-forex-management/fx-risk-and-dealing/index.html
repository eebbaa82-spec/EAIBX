FX risk, dealing and forwards

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Run the bank's foreign currency position within the NBE limit, price customer deals and forwards, and control the dealing room.

In this module

  1. 1.The FX regime since July 2024
  2. 2.Quotes, spreads and cross rates
  3. 3.The net open position and the NBE limit
  4. 4.Value at risk and the capital link
  5. 5.Forward contracts under FXD/04/2026
  6. 6.Dealing room conduct and controls
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how the market-based FX regime works since July 2024 and what has changed since
  • Quote spot and cross rates and price customer FX deals
  • Compute the net open position and test it against the NBE limit
  • Estimate value at risk and explain the market risk capital link
  • Price a forward contract and apply dealing room controls
