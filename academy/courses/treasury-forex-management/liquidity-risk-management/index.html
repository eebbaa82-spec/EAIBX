← Treasury and Forex Management

Liquidity risk management

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Forecast cash flows, set behavioral assumptions, stress the balance sheet and prepare a contingency funding plan that works in Ethiopian markets.

In this module

  1. 1.Where Birr liquidity comes from
  2. 2.The maturity ladder and the funding gap
  3. 3.Behavioral assumptions: core and volatile deposits
  4. 4.Liquidity stress testing
  5. 5.LCR and NSFR as concepts
  6. 6.The contingency funding plan
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Map the Birr liquidity sources available to an Ethiopian bank, from deposits to NBE facilities
  • Build a maturity ladder and identify the funding gap
  • Set and defend behavioral assumptions for deposits and loan drawdowns
  • Run a liquidity stress test and compute an LCR and NSFR on a course basis
  • Design a contingency funding plan with triggers and actions
