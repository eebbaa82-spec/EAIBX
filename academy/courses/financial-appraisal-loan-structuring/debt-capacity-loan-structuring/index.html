Debt capacity and loan structuring

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

A good project can still be a bad loan if repayments start too early or rise too fast; learn to shape the schedule to the project's cash flow.

In this module

  1. 1.DSCR, year by year
  2. 2.The structuring levers
  3. 3.Repayment shapes
  4. 4.Three structures for Sebeta
  5. 5.Interest during grace, fees and equity
  6. 6.Covenants and exposure limits
  7. 7.Case: structuring Hawassa Dairy's loan
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute DSCR for every year of the loan and judge it by the minimum year
  • Use grace, tenor, repayment shape, interest during grace, equity, fees and covenants as structuring levers
  • Compare equal principal, grace and sculpted structures for the same loan
  • Check a structure against the manual's tenor and grace rules and NBE exposure limits
