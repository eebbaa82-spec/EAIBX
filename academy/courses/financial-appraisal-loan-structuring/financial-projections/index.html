Financial projections

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Build the projected income statement and cash flow line by line, so every figure traces back to an assumption the committee can question.

In this module

  1. 1.The chain of projections
  2. 2.Revenue, variable costs and fixed costs
  3. 3.Depreciation: a cost, but not a cash payment
  4. 4.Interest, tax and the loss year
  5. 5.Cash for debt service and working capital
  6. 6.Common projection errors to catch
  7. 7.Case: Hawassa Dairy's projections
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build revenue from capacity, utilization and price, with a realistic ramp-up
  • Separate variable and fixed costs and compute EBITDA, EBIT, interest, tax and net profit
  • Explain why depreciation is added back and derive cash flow available for debt service
  • Spot the common projection errors that inflate a project's case
