Investment appraisal: NPV, IRR, payback and break-even

Module 03 / 5 · 60 min

Judge whether the project as a whole creates value, separately from how the loan is structured, and how far sales can fall before it stops covering its costs.

In this module

  1. 1.Project cash flow: is the project good?
  2. 2.NPV and the choice of discount rate
  3. 3.IRR: the project's own rate of return
  4. 4.Payback: how fast is the money back?
  5. 5.Break-even and cash break-even
  6. 6.Case: is Hawassa Dairy worth financing?
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Build project (unlevered) cash flow and explain why interest is left out
  • Compute and interpret NPV, IRR and payback, and choose a defensible discount rate
  • Compute accounting and cash break-even and compare them with planned volume
  • Give a one-line verdict on whether a project is worth financing
