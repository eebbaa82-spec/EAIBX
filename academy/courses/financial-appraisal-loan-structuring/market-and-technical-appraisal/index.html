Market and technical appraisal

Module 01 / 5 · 55 min · Free preview

Test whether the plant can make the product and whether buyers will take it at the planned price, before any number goes into the projections.

In this module

  1. 1.Every number starts with two questions
  2. 2.Demand: current consumption, growth and its source
  3. 3.Supply and the market share test
  4. 4.Price, import parity and the channels to market
  5. 5.Technical appraisal: can the plant make it?
  6. 6.Case: Hawassa Dairy's market and technical case
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Test demand, supply and the market share a project claims against independent evidence
  • Check the planned price against competitors, import parity and price history
  • Run the core technical checks on technology, utilities, raw materials and the implementation schedule
  • Turn each weakness in a feasibility study into a specific evidence request or loan condition
