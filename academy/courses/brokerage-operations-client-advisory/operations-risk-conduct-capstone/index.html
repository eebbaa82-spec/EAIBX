← Brokerage Operations and Client Advisory

Operations control, firm risk and conduct

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Run a brokerage with sound controls: reconcile trades, handle errors, keep the firm's capital and 40% liquid assets in line, prevent conflicts and market abuse, handle complaints, and defend three advisory files to compliance.

In this module

  1. 1.Order management and trade reconciliation
  2. 2.Errors, operational risk and business continuity
  3. 3.The firm's capital, liquidity and dealer risk
  4. 4.Conflicts of interest, churning and front-running
  5. 5.Complaints handling and ECMA reporting
  6. 6.Capstone: defend three advisory files
  7. 7.Work assignment: review your own firm's controls
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Design trade reconciliation, error handling and business continuity controls for a brokerage
  • Test the firm's liquid asset ratio under Directive No. 980/2024 and manage dealer inventory and credit risk
  • Identify conflicts of interest, churning and front-running and handle complaints and ECMA reporting
  • Defend documented recommendations for three clients and review your own firm's operations controls

Built on

  • Erudite Africa course specification 2.e: Brokerage Operations and Client Advisory, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • Erudite Africa: Ethiopian Context programme overview and regulatory register (October 2026)
  • Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; ECMA Directives No. 980/2024 (licensing and supervision) and No. 996/2024 (fees)
  • ESX Rulebook (December 2024)
  • NBE Directive No. FXD/01/2024 as amended, and the NBE Monetary Policy Committee release No. 7 (July 2026)
  • AML/CFT and personal data protection laws as reported in the regulatory register (numbers to be confirmed)

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
