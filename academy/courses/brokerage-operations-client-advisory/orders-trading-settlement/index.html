Orders, trading and settlement on the ESX

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Take an order from the client's instruction through the ESX trading day to settlement in the depository, and deal with partial fills, failed trades and corporate actions.

In this module

  1. 1.How the ESX market is organised
  2. 2.Order types and the order ticket
  3. 3.The ESX trading day
  4. 4.Priority, partial fills and fair allocation
  5. 5.Clearing and settlement in the depository
  6. 6.Failed trades and corporate actions
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the ESX boards, trading day and lot size set by the ESX Rulebook
  • Choose and explain the right order type for a client and record a complete order ticket
  • Follow a trade from execution through clearing to settlement in CSD accounts
  • Handle a failed trade and process a corporate action such as a rights issue
