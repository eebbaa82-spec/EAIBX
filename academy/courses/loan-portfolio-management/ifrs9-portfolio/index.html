IFRS 9 in the portfolio

Module 04 / 5 · 60 min

Stage the book, recognise significant increases in credit risk, compute expected credit loss with forward-looking scenarios, and reconcile IFRS 9 with SBB/90/2024 minimum provisions.

In this module

  1. 1.Three stages of credit quality
  2. 2.Significant increase in credit risk
  3. 3.Expected credit loss: PD x LGD x EAD
  4. 4.Forward-looking scenarios
  5. 5.Reconciling IFRS 9 and SBB/90/2024
  6. 6.Explaining the numbers to management
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Assign loans to IFRS 9 Stage 1, 2 or 3 and explain the effect on the loss allowance
  • Identify significant increase in credit risk from quantitative and qualitative triggers
  • Compute expected credit loss as PD x LGD x EAD and weight it across forward-looking scenarios
  • Reconcile IFRS 9 allowances with SBB/90/2024 minimum provisions and the regulatory reserve
