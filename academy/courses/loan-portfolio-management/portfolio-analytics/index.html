Portfolio analytics: vintages, migration and concentration

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Read the whole loan book as a portfolio: quality indicators, vintage, migration and roll-rate analysis, concentration and the quality of the data underneath.

In this module

  1. 1.From the single loan to the whole book
  2. 2.Vintage analysis: judging loans by their birth year
  3. 3.Migration matrices and roll rates
  4. 4.Concentration: the Herfindahl index and the top 20
  5. 5.Connected borrowers hidden in the data
  6. 6.Data quality: the analysis is only as good as the file
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute and interpret the NPL ratio, past-due buckets, provision coverage and sector mix of a loan book
  • Build vintage, migration and roll-rate tables and use them to forecast new NPLs
  • Measure concentration with the Herfindahl index and the top 20 borrower share
  • Find connected borrowers and data errors that distort portfolio figures
