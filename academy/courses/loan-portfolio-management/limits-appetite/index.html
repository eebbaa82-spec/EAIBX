Limits and risk appetite

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Turn portfolio analysis into limits: sector, product, single obligor and group limits aligned with NBE large-exposure and related-party rules, and a capital-based credit budget now that the credit growth cap has gone.

In this module

  1. 1.What a credit risk appetite statement says
  2. 2.Large exposures and groups of connected counterparties
  3. 3.Related-party limits
  4. 4.Sector, product and region limits
  5. 5.Capital as the credit budget
  6. 6.Lending after the credit growth cap
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Draft a credit risk appetite statement with measurable limits
  • Test exposures against SBB/87/2024 large-exposure and SBB/88/2024 related-party limits
  • Set sector, product and region limits from portfolio data
  • Use SBB/95/2025 risk weights to build a capital-based credit budget after the removal of the credit growth cap
