Workout and restructuring

Module 03 / 5 · 70 min

Design workout strategies that preserve value: viability assessment, restructuring options, NBE treatment of restructured loans, debt-to-asset swaps, foreclosure and write-off.

In this module

  1. 1.From watch list to workout
  2. 2.Is the business viable?
  3. 3.Restructuring options
  4. 4.How NBE treats restructured loans
  5. 5.Debt-to-asset swaps and foreclosed assets
  6. 6.Foreclosure, court and write-off
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Move a problem loan from watch list to workout through the bank's escalation steps
  • Assess viability and compare the value of rescue with foreclosure
  • Apply SBB/90/2024 rules on non-accrual, cross-default and restructured loans
  • Manage debt-to-asset swaps, foreclosed assets and write-off
