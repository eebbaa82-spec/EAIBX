Remittances and Ethiopia

Module 01 / 5 · 45 min · Free preview

Why remittances matter to Ethiopian households and to the country's foreign exchange, where they come from, and why informal channels shrank after the July 2024 reform.

In this module

  1. 1.What a remittance is
  2. 2.Where the money comes from
  3. 3.Why remittances matter for foreign exchange
  4. 4.Informal channels and why people used them
  5. 5.What changed after July 2024
  6. 6.The running case: a week at the Piassa desk
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Define inward and outward remittances and the parties to a transfer
  • Name the main sending corridors and the kinds of senders in each
  • Explain why remittances matter for households and for the banking system's FX supply
  • Explain why many families used informal channels and why that changed after July 2024

Built on

  • Erudite Africa course specification: 1.m International Money Transfers and Remittances, Level 1 Beginner (Ethiopian context module, October 2026)
  • NBE Directive No. FXD/01/2024 and its amendment FXD/04/2026; NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025
  • National Payment System Proclamation No. 718/2011 as amended by No. 1282/2023, and NBE Directive No. ONPS/09/2023 (as reported)
  • AML/CFT Proclamation No. 780/2013 (as reported), the NBE KYC/CDD directive and FATF Recommendation 16
  • NBE Directive No. FCP/01/2020 on financial consumer protection (as reported)
  • Erudite Africa training module: Domestic Banking Operations (October 2026), for diaspora account and incoming remittance procedures

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
