Fundamental analysis and valuation

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Work from the economy to the industry to the company, read the annual report of an Ethiopian bank or insurer, and value its shares with dividends, multiples and an eye on corporate actions.

In this module

  1. 1.Economy, industry, company
  2. 2.Reading Ethiopian bank and insurer annual reports
  3. 3.The dividend discount and Gordon growth model
  4. 4.P/E, P/B and dividend policy
  5. 5.Corporate actions and their effect on price
  6. 6.A basic fundamental analysis of a listed company
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Carry out a top-down economy, industry and company analysis for an Ethiopian listed company
  • Read the key lines of an Ethiopian bank's and insurer's annual report
  • Value a share with the Gordon growth model, P/E and P/B, and explain dividend policy in Ethiopian share companies
  • Explain the effect of rights issues, bonus issues, splits, mergers and buybacks on price and holders

  • NBE financial sector data for FY2024/25 and ESX and ECMA market data, as compiled in Investopia sector research (October 2026)

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
