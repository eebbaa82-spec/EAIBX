← Investment Analysis and Portfolio Management

Portfolio theory, asset pricing and fixed income risk

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Optimise a constrained portfolio, estimate the cost of equity for Ethiopia with short histories, and manage interest rate and credit risk in Birr bonds and sukuk.

In this module

  1. 1.Mean-variance optimisation with constraints
  2. 2.CAPM and beta with short histories
  3. 3.Cost of equity for Ethiopia: the country risk premium
  4. 4.Interest rate risk: duration and convexity
  5. 5.Credit spreads, ratings, covenants and security
  6. 6.Sukuk for interest-free investors
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Set up a mean-variance optimisation in a spreadsheet with Ethiopian regulatory constraints
  • Apply CAPM, estimate beta from short ESX histories and build a cost of equity with a country risk premium
  • Calculate duration, modified duration and convexity and estimate the effect of an NBR change
  • Assess credit spreads, covenants and security, and explain sukuk structures for interest-free investors
