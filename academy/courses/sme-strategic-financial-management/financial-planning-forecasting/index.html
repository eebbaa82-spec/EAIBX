Financial Planning and Forecasting

Module 01 / 6 · 65 min · Free preview

Turn a growth plan into linked three-year projections, build revenue from real drivers and test the plan under base, downside and upside scenarios.

In this module

  1. 1.From strategy to numbers: the planning cycle
  2. 2.Business projections: three statements that must agree
  3. 3.Revenue forecasting from real drivers
  4. 4.Forecasting costs, working capital and tax
  5. 5.Scenario analysis: base, downside and upside
  6. 6.Presenting the forecast to a bank or investor
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain how strategy, assumptions, the financial model, the budget and monitoring fit into one planning cycle
  • Build integrated projections in which the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow agree
  • Forecast revenue from volume, price and capacity drivers and test it for realism
  • Run base, downside and upside scenarios and read what they mean for cash and debt service
  • Present a forecast to a bank or investor with a clear assumptions book
