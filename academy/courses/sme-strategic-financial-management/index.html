Plan, finance, protect and measure a growing Ethiopian SME, and make it investor-ready

Build a three-year financial model with scenarios, prepare investor-ready financials, compare bank loans, equity, leasing and development finance, manage credit, liquidity, FX and fraud risk, and run the business on a KPI dashboard and balanced scorecard.

The case you will follow

Adama Pack Industries PLC, a growing carton maker in Adama

Adama Pack Industries PLC is a fictional private limited company in Adama that makes corrugated cartons for food processors, beverage companies and fruit and flower exporters. It has four shareholders and about 140 staff, sold Birr 120 million in FY2025/26 with EBITDA of Birr 20.4 million, and has a Bank A term loan and overdraft. Its one line produced 8,000 tonnes against a 9,000 tonne capacity. Managing director Ato Henok Tesfaye wants a second Birr 30 million line, and finance manager Wro. Selamawit Girma leads the work: a three-year model with scenarios, investor readiness and due diligence with Meridian Impact Fund, a Birr 24 million hire purchase from Entoto Capital Goods Leasing S.C. alongside Birr 20 million of new equity, a risk and controls review, a rebuilt Excel model and dashboard, and a balanced scorecard for the expansion years. Every figure in the lessons comes from the same model, which is also in the course workbook.

Capstone project

A complete financial management system and investor-ready financial package for an SME

Develop a complete financial management system and investor-ready financial package for an SME: your own business, a client or the Adama Pack case. Using the course workbook, you will build a three-year model with scenarios, a financing plan, a KPI dashboard and balanced scorecard, and a data room checklist, and present them as the package a bank or investor would receive.

  1. 1Enter three years of actual results (or the Adama Pack Year 0 figures) and the drivers for three projected years on the Assumptions sheet, with the source and owner of each driver
  2. 2Complete the base, downside and upside drivers, use the scenario switch to produce linked projected statements for each case, and show that the balance check is zero in every year
  3. 3Build the financing plan: sources and uses, the loan and lease schedules and a lease versus buy comparison at the after-tax cost of borrowing, then choose the financing mix and explain why
  4. 4Prepare the valuation summary and normalized EBITDA, and calculate the DSCR, covenant headroom and break-even revenue in each scenario
  5. 5Set up the KPI dashboard and the balanced scorecard with targets taken from the base case, traffic-light thresholds and owners
  6. 6Complete the investor data room checklist, including a tax health check, and list the gaps with owners and dates
  7. 7Write a two-page financial narrative and present the package to your facilitator or board as you would to Bank A or an investor

What you hand in

  • The completed workbook: three-year model with working scenario switch, loan and lease schedules, lease versus buy and valuation summary, all checks at zero
  • A KPI dashboard and balanced scorecard with targets, owners and thresholds
  • A financing plan with sources and uses and the recommended mix, with DSCR in each scenario
  • The investor data room checklist with tax health check results and a two-page financial narrative
