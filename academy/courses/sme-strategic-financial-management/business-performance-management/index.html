Business Performance Management

Module 06 / 6 · 60 min

Run the business on a balanced scorecard and enterprise health indicators, plan growth the balance sheet can carry and report to the board in a steady rhythm.

In this module

  1. 1.From strategy to measures
  2. 2.Balanced scorecards
  3. 3.Enterprise health indicators
  4. 4.Growth planning: how fast can we grow?
  5. 5.Retaining profit, capital increases and dividends
  6. 6.Board reporting and the review rhythm
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Link strategy to a small set of financial and non-financial KPIs with owners and targets
  • Build a balanced scorecard across financial, customer, internal process and learning perspectives
  • Track enterprise health indicators for liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency and compliance
  • Plan growth with the sustainable growth rate and decide between retaining profit and paying dividends
  • Set a monthly, quarterly and annual review rhythm with the board
