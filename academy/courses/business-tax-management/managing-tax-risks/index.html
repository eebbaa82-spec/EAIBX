Managing Tax Risks

Module 05 / 6 · 60 min

Map where tax risk arises, put simple internal controls and a documentation system in place, understand penalties and waivers, and manage disputes through objection, conciliation and appeal.

In this module

  1. 1.Where tax risk comes from
  2. 2.Internal controls for tax
  3. 3.A tax documentation system
  4. 4.Penalties, interest and how to stay compliant
  5. 5.Managing tax disputes
  6. 6.Rulings, clearance and staying ahead
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Identify the main sources of tax risk in an Ethiopian business, NGO or MFI
  • Design internal controls and a tax documentation system
  • Explain interest, penalties and the waiver rules, and the liability of managers and finance heads
  • Manage disputes through objection, conciliation and appeal within the deadlines

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training curriculum: Taxation Fundamentals for Ethiopian Businesses, Level 2 Intermediate, Business Tax Management (October 2026)
  • Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016 as amended by Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025, and Council of Ministers Income Tax Regulation No. 410/2017 as amended; article references for No. 1395/2025 per KPMG, EY, PwC and Grant Thornton summaries
  • Value Added Tax Proclamation No. 1341/2024, VAT Regulation No. 570/2025, VAT Registration Directive No. 1104/2025, Directive No. 1021/2024 and Electronic Invoicing System Administration Directive No. 1142/2026
  • Federal Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016 as amended by Tax Administration (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1434/2026, and Penalty Waiver Directive No. 189/2025
  • Financial Reporting Proclamation No. 847/2014, Private Organization Employees' Pension Proclamation No. 1268/2022 and Investment Incentive Regulation No. 586/2026

Examples and companies are fictional. Tax rates and rules are shown as they stood when the course was written; always check the current proclamations, directives and guidance from the Ministry of Revenues before filing, and take professional advice for your own case.
