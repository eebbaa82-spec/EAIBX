Digital essentials and cyber hygiene

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Work productively with files, email, meetings and core banking screens, and protect yourself and the bank from the most common cyber attacks.

In this module

  1. 1.File management that colleagues can follow
  2. 2.Email, collaboration tools and core banking screens
  3. 3.Remote meetings and Amharic and English typing
  4. 4.Passwords and multi-factor authentication
  5. 5.Phishing and social engineering
  6. 6.Safe mobile use and reporting incidents
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Name, organize and share work files so colleagues always find the current version
  • Use email, collaboration tools, remote meetings and core banking screens professionally and securely
  • Type and store Amharic and English text in a form others can search and reuse
  • Protect accounts and devices with strong passphrases, multi-factor authentication and safe mobile habits
  • Recognize phishing and social engineering and report an incident quickly
