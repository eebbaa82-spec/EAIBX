Data protection and the capstone dashboard

Module 05 / 5 · 65 min

Handle customer data within Ethiopia's data protection law, share only what is needed, and complete the branch dashboard capstone.

In this module

  1. 1.What the data protection law asks of us
  2. 2.Consent and customer rights
  3. 3.National ID, credit data and sensitive fields
  4. 4.Recognizing and reporting a breach
  5. 5.Anonymize and aggregate before you share
  6. 6.Capstone: the Hawassa Tabor branch dashboard
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Apply the main principles of the Personal Data Protection Proclamation to everyday work
  • Obtain and respect customer consent and handle customer requests about their data
  • Recognize a personal data breach and report it through the bank's procedure
  • Anonymize or aggregate data before a report leaves its intended audience
  • Complete, check and present the one-page branch dashboard

  • Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, Electronic Transaction Proclamation No. 1205/2020, Computer Crime Proclamation No. 958/2016 and National ID Proclamation No. 1284/2023 (as reported)
