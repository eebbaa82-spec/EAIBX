Designing an anchor-led programme

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Map where cash gets stuck in a value chain, choose the right anchor and design how suppliers join, qualify and get paid.

In this module

  1. 1.Where the cash gets stuck in a value chain
  2. 2.The SCF product family and how it differs from a loan
  3. 3.Choosing the anchor
  4. 4.Sizing the Meskerem programme
  5. 5.Supplier onboarding and eligibility
  6. 6.Programme documents and platform choice
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Map an Ethiopian value chain and locate the finance gaps and risks at each stage
  • Distinguish the main supply chain finance products from a plain working capital loan
  • Select an anchor and size a programme from its spend, terms and supplier base
  • Set supplier onboarding and eligibility rules and choose a delivery platform
