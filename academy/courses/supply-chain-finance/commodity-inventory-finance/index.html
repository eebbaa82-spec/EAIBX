Commodity and inventory finance

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Lend against goods you control: register the security, contract a collateral manager, monitor the warehouse, margin the loan and use ECX warehouse receipts.

In this module

  1. 1.The legal basis for inventory and receipt finance
  2. 2.Collateral management agreements
  3. 3.Warehouse monitoring and stock reconciliation
  4. 4.Valuation, advance rates and margin calls
  5. 5.ECX warehouse receipts: pledge to foreclosure
  6. 6.Running case: financing barley before it reaches Meskerem
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe how security over inventory and warehouse receipts is created, registered and enforced in Ethiopia
  • Set up a collateral management agreement and a warehouse monitoring routine
  • Value pledged commodities, set advance rates and calculate a margin call
  • Use ECX electronic warehouse receipts for finance, from pledge to foreclosure
