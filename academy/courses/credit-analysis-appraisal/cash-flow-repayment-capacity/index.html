← Level 2: Credit Analysis and Appraisal

Cash flow, working capital and repayment capacity

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

Size a facility from the borrower's cash cycle and cash flow, not from what the customer asks for or what the collateral can carry.

In this module

  1. 1.Cash flow from operations
  2. 2.The working capital gap and the owner's margin
  3. 3.Sizing Bole's overdraft
  4. 4.The debt service coverage ratio
  5. 5.Can Bole service the new term loan?
  6. 6.Designing repayment, grace and tenor
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute cash flow from operations and explain why a profitable firm can run short of cash
  • Estimate the working capital gap and size an overdraft with an owner's margin
  • Calculate and interpret the debt service coverage ratio for each year of a term loan
  • Design a repayment schedule, grace period and tenor that match the borrower's cash flow
