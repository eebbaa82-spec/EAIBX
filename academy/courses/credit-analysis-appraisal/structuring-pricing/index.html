Structuring and pricing the facility

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Shape security, the owner's stake, conditions and price so that the deal fits the risk the analysis has found.

In this module

  1. 1.Collateral coverage and haircuts
  2. 2.Collateral types and their rules
  3. 3.The owner's equity contribution
  4. 4.Conditions precedent and covenants
  5. 5.Pricing to the risk
  6. 6.Demonstration: Bole's package
  7. 7.Case study: Abay Construction's excavator
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compute collateral coverage after haircuts and apply revaluation rules
  • Set and verify the owner's equity contribution for project, vehicle and machinery loans
  • Draft conditions precedent and financial, information and operating covenants
  • Build a loan price from cost of funds, operating cost, margin, term premium and risk premium
