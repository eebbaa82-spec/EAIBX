← Level 2: Credit Analysis and Appraisal

Account performance, the credit report and the committee

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Pull the analysis into one written recommendation and defend it before a credit committee that asks hard questions.

In this module

  1. 1.Reading account performance
  2. 2.When an overdraft goes hard-core
  3. 3.The credit analysis report
  4. 4.A weak and a strong executive summary
  5. 5.Facing the committee
  6. 6.After the decision: notification to disbursement
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Measure account performance across overdraft, merchandise, L/C, pre-shipment, guarantee and term loan facilities
  • Write a credit analysis report in the standard eleven-section format
  • Draft a one-page executive summary that states a clear recommendation, the key risk and its mitigant
  • Defend a recommendation under committee questioning, including sensitivity and policy exceptions
