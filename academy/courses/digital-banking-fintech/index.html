Digital Banking and Fintech Integration

Channels, payment flows, rules, onboarding and fraud in Ethiopian digital finance

A practical foundation course on Ethiopia's digital finance ecosystem: how digital payments flow, the rules frontline staff must apply, safe Fayda-based onboarding, complaint handling and fraud prevention.

Start the course

0 of 5 modules completed

What you will be able to do

  • Describe the digital channels and players in Ethiopian finance and how a digital payment flows
  • Explain the bank's digital products to customers and onboard them safely
  • Apply the frontline rules on licensed activities, limits, agents, consumer protection and data privacy
  • Recognize common digital fraud and cyber threats and respond in the right order
  • Handle a failed-transaction complaint from first contact to resolution

The case you will follow

Kedir's stall in Merkato

Kedir Abdella sells household goods from a stall in Merkato, Addis Ababa, and takes about Birr 60,000 a day, almost all in cash. Hana Girma, a customer service officer at Bank A's Merkato branch, onboards him to mobile banking and a merchant QR code using Fayda e-KYC. Two weeks later, a buyer's Birr 4,800 QR payment times out and Hana must handle the complaint; later, Kedir's phone suddenly loses service in a likely SIM swap. The course ends with the Merkato role play, scored against a rubric.

For
Branch, product, IT and operations staff of Ethiopian banks and microfinance institutions who serve or support digital channel customers. No prerequisites.
Study time
About 5 hours online, in 5 modules (16 hours in the classroom version)

Assessment

Certificate
Erudite Africa Center of Excellence certificate of completion
Access

The 5 modules

  1. Module 01Free previewThe Ethiopian digital finance ecosystemThe channels and players in Ethiopian digital finance: bank apps and USSD, wallets, the national switch, cards, ATMs, POS and QR payments.6 lessons · 55 min · quiz
  2. Module 02How digital payments workWhat happens behind a tap or a scan: account-to-account, wallet-to-bank and merchant payments, and how institutions settle through EthSwitch and the NBE.6 lessons · 55 min · quiz
  3. Module 03Regulation for frontline staffThe rules a frontline officer must know: who is licensed to do what, transaction limits and KYC tiers, agents, consumer protection and customer data.6 lessons · 55 min · quiz
  4. Module 04Digital onboarding and customer supportOnboarding customers safely with Fayda-based e-KYC, activating apps and PINs, solving common problems and handling complaints, including the Merkato role play.6 lessons · 65 min · quiz
  5. Module 05Fraud and cyber hygieneThe fraud and cyber threats in digital channels, what to do when fraud happens, the bank's awareness duties and the course capstone role play.7 lessons · 65 min · quiz
  6. Officer simulationA week as digital banking officer at Bank A, Merkato branchWork as digital banking officer: 11 real-life items, decisions with consequences and a hand-over note. Pass with 70% to qualify for the certificate.
  7. Final examFinal exam and certificate20 applied questions across all 5 modules. Pass with 70% to download your certificate.

Built on

  • Erudite Africa Ethiopian context module, topic 1.h Digital Banking and Fintech Integration, Level 1 Beginner (October 2026)
  • NBE Proclamation No. 1359/2025, NBE Directive ONPS/09/2023 and NBE Instruction FCPED/142/26, as cited in the lessons
  • National Payment System Proclamation No. 718/2011 as amended by No. 1282/2023, and the payment system operator, consumer protection, electronic transaction, data protection and national ID texts marked reported in the lessons
  • NBE Risk Management Guidelines (May 2010)
  • National Financial Inclusion Strategy II (2021 to 2025) and the Digital Ethiopia 2025 strategy

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
