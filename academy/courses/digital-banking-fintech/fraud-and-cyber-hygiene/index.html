Fraud and cyber hygiene

Module 05 / 5 · 65 min

The fraud and cyber threats in digital channels, what to do when fraud happens, the bank's awareness duties and the course capstone role play.

In this module you will learn to

  • Recognize SIM swap, social engineering, phishing, agent fraud and insider risk
  • Respond to a fraud report in the right order: block, record, escalate, report
  • Explain the customer fraud awareness duties in NBE instruction FCPED/142/26
  • Score a digital onboarding and complaint role play against a rubric
