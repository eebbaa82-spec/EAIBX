Project Cost and the Financing Plan

Module 03 / 5 · 60 min

Rebuild a project's cost from its parts so nothing is missing and nothing is inflated, and check that the owners really carry at least 30% of it.

In this module

  1. 1.The eight components of project cost
  2. 2.Contingency and interest during construction
  3. 3.Sebeta's cost and financing plan
  4. 4.The equity rule and what counts as equity
  5. 5.Other sources: supplier credit, external loans and syndicates
  6. 6.Case study: Adama Cement Block Factory
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • List the eight project cost components and the evidence for each
  • Calculate contingency and interest during construction
  • Build a financing plan and test it against the 30% equity rule
  • Judge whether cash and in-kind contributions count as equity
