Reading a Feasibility Study

Module 02 / 5 · 55 min

A feasibility study is the sponsor's sales document, so the officer tests every important assumption against independent evidence.

In this module

  1. 1.The study is a sales document
  2. 2.The parts of a feasibility study
  3. 3.Testing the Sebeta study claim by claim
  4. 4.Red flags in the Bishoftu Poultry study
  5. 5.Why new plants ramp up
  6. 6.Verifying the study: interview, site and credit checks
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Name the parts of a feasibility study and what each should show
  • Ask the right evidence questions for market, technical, financial, legal and environmental claims
  • Spot common red flags such as no ramp-up, frozen input prices and unproven price premiums
  • Use the interview, site visit and credit information checks to verify a study
