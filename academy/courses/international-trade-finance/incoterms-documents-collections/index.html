Incoterms 2020, trade documents and documentary collections

Module 02 / 5 · 60 min

Match the Incoterms rule to a landlocked country's routes, read the main trade documents and run a documentary collection under URC 522.

In this module

  1. 1.Incoterms 2020: who does what
  2. 2.Choosing rules for a landlocked country, and insurance
  3. 3.Transport documents
  4. 4.Commercial, official and ECX documents
  5. 5.Documentary collections under URC 522
  6. 6.Bills of exchange and Ethiopian collection practice
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the eleven Incoterms 2020 rules and choose those that suit Ethiopian cargo routes
  • State the insurance requirements under CIF and CIP
  • Describe the purpose and key data of the main transport, commercial and official documents, including ECX documents
  • Run a D/P or D/A collection under URC 522 and explain the roles of the remitting and collecting banks

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 1.d International Trade Finance Instruments, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: FXD/01/2024, FXD/04/2026, FXD/05/2026, FVD/01/2026, SBB/96/2025, SBB/99/2026, and the MPC No. 7 release (July 2026)
  • Ethiopian Investment Board Directive No. 1083/2025 and Special Economic Zone Proclamation No. 1322/2024
  • ICC rules: UCP 600, ISBP 821, URC 522, URDG 758, ISP98, URR 725, eUCP and Incoterms 2020
  • Commercial Code of Ethiopia (negotiable instruments), cited as reported

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
