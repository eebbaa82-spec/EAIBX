Public offers, market conduct and AML/CFT

Module 03 / 5 · 65 min

Know when an offer needs ECMA registration and a prospectus, recognize insider dealing, manipulation and conflicts, apply AML/CFT due diligence, and classify activities as permitted, licensable or prohibited.

In this module

  1. 1.When an offer becomes public
  2. 2.What a prospectus must tell investors
  3. 3.Insider dealing, manipulation and false statements
  4. 4.Conflicts of interest and client assets
  5. 5.AML/CFT: customer due diligence and beneficial ownership
  6. 6.Exercise: permitted, licensable or prohibited
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain when an offer is public and what registration and a prospectus require
  • Distinguish a private placement from a public offer using the 100 investor ceiling
  • Recognize insider dealing, market manipulation and false statements
  • Apply client asset segregation and conflict rules
  • Carry out customer due diligence and beneficial ownership checks

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 2.b Ethiopian Capital Markets Law and Regulation, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; ECMA Directives No. 980/2024 (licensing and supervision) and No. 996/2024 (fees)
  • ESX Rulebook (December 2024), Main Board and Growth Board listing rules
  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/89/2024, SBB/91/2024, SBB/92/2024
  • ECMA directives reported in the programme register, texts to confirm: No. 1009/2024 (exchanges and OTC), No. 1030/2024 (public offers), collective investment scheme directives
  • Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020 and Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
