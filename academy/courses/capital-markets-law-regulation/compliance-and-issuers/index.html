Compliance programs and issuer obligations

Module 04 / 5 · 70 min

Build and run a compliance program for a licensed firm, meet ECMA's reporting calendar, and advise issuers, including widely held unlisted share companies, on disclosure, governance and listing.

In this module

  1. 1.Building the compliance program
  2. 2.Regulatory returns and the reporting calendar
  3. 3.Inspections, sanctions and the gap review
  4. 4.Issuer obligations: prospectus liability and continuous disclosure
  5. 5.Corporate governance of listed issuers and banks
  6. 6.Legacy unlisted share companies and bank share transfers
  7. 7.Can Bank A meet the ESX Main Board test?
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Design a compliance manual, monitoring plan and whistleblowing channel
  • Meet the reporting deadlines and record-keeping rules of Directive No. 980/2024
  • Advise an issuer on prospectus liability and continuous disclosure
  • Apply NBE governance and shareholder approval rules to a bank preparing to list
  • Test an issuer against the ESX Main Board listing criteria
← Previous modulePublic offers, market conduct and AML/CFTNext module →Market abuse enforcement, funds and the listing roadmap

Built on

  • Erudite Africa training module: Ethiopian Context, topic 2.b Ethiopian Capital Markets Law and Regulation, Levels 1 and 2 (October 2026)
  • Capital Market Proclamation No. 1248/2021; ECMA Directives No. 980/2024 (licensing and supervision) and No. 996/2024 (fees)
  • ESX Rulebook (December 2024), Main Board and Growth Board listing rules
  • NBE directives as listed in the lessons: SBB/89/2024, SBB/91/2024, SBB/92/2024
  • ECMA directives reported in the programme register, texts to confirm: No. 1009/2024 (exchanges and OTC), No. 1030/2024 (public offers), collective investment scheme directives
  • Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020 and Banking Business Proclamation No. 1360/2025

Examples and companies are fictional. Bank practices are shown as typical Ethiopian practice, not as any one bank's policy. Always apply your own institution's approved policy and the current NBE directives.
