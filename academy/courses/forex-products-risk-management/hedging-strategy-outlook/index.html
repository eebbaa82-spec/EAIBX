← Foreign Exchange (Forex) Products and Risk Management

Hedging strategy, policy and market outlook

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Design a corporate hedging policy, choose hedge ratios that balance cost and protection, and explain rate expectations responsibly.

In this module

  1. 1.Designing a hedging policy for a corporate
  2. 2.Hedge ratios and layering
  3. 3.Cost versus protection
  4. 4.Hedge accounting in brief
  5. 5.Reading NBE, IMF and market data
  6. 6.Explaining rate expectations to clients
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Set out the parts of a corporate FX hedging policy
  • Choose and justify a hedge ratio for committed and forecast exposures
  • Compare outcomes of different hedge ratios across rate scenarios
  • Give an overview of hedge accounting under IFRS 9
  • Use NBE, IMF and market data to explain rate expectations without making promises
