← Foreign Exchange (Forex) Products and Risk Management

Bank-side risk and the capstone hedging proposal

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Control the credit, market and conduct risks the bank takes when it sells FX products, then present a complete hedging proposal for the running case.

In this module

  1. 1.Counterparty credit limits for forwards
  2. 2.The bank's net open position
  3. 3.Suitability and mis-selling risk
  4. 4.AML/CFT and conduct in FX business
  5. 5.Capstone: Gefersa's hedging proposal
  6. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Set a counterparty credit limit for forwards using replacement cost and an add-on
  • Measure the bank's net open position against the SBB/96/2025 limit and cover client deals
  • Apply suitability checks and AML/CFT controls to FX products
  • Prepare and defend a client hedging proposal for the capstone
