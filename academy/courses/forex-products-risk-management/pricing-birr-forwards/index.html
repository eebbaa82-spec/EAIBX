Forwards: pricing, documentation and settlement

Module 02 / 5 · 70 min

Price a birr forward from interest rate parity, turn it into a client rate, and document and settle it correctly.

In this module

  1. 1.What a forward is, and why it is new in Ethiopia
  2. 2.Interest rate parity: where the forward rate comes from
  3. 3.Forward points with high birr rates
  4. 4.From mid rate to client price
  5. 5.Documentation and settlement
  6. 6.When the date or the amount changes
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what a forward contract is and what FXD/04/2026 allows
  • Derive a forward rate from interest rate parity and compute forward points
  • Build a client forward price from mid rate, spread and credit charge
  • Describe the documents, settlement steps and what happens when dates change
