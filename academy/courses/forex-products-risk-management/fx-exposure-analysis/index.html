← Foreign Exchange (Forex) Products and Risk Management

Mapping a client's FX exposure

Module 01 / 5 · 65 min · Free preview

Find, measure and explain the foreign exchange risk inside a corporate customer's cash flows and balance sheet under Ethiopia's market-based regime.

In this module

  1. 1.The FX regime your clients now live in
  2. 2.Transaction, translation and economic exposure
  3. 3.Building a currency cash flow map
  4. 4.Natural hedges before financial hedges
  5. 5.FX debt on balance sheets after the 2024 depreciation
  6. 6.The running case: Gefersa's exposure in birr
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the post-2024 FX rules that shape what customers can and cannot do
  • Separate transaction, translation and economic exposure in a corporate client
  • Build a currency cash flow map and compute net exposure by period
  • Measure the effect of the 2024 depreciation on FX debt held on a balance sheet
  • Quantify the running case exposure under several rate outcomes
