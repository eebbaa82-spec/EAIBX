Risk basics and the risks a bank runs

Module 01 / 5 · 60 min · Free preview

Understand what risk is, why banks fail, and the main risk types an Ethiopian bank faces, each with a local example.

In this module

  1. 1.Risk, uncertainty and return
  2. 2.Why banks fail
  3. 3.Credit and concentration risk
  4. 4.Market risk and liquidity risk
  5. 5.Operational, compliance, IT and cyber risk
  6. 6.Strategic, reputational and climate risk
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain risk, uncertainty and the link between risk and return
  • Describe the common reasons banks fail and relate them to Ethiopian experience
  • Name and explain credit, market, liquidity, operational, compliance, IT and cyber, strategic, reputational and climate risk
  • Give an Ethiopian example of each main risk type
