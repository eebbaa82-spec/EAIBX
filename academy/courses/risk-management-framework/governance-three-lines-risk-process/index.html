Governance, the three lines and the risk process

Module 02 / 5 · 65 min

See who owns which risk decision, how the three lines work together, and how to identify, rate and record risks in a risk register.

In this module

  1. 1.The board, the risk committee and the CRO
  2. 2.What the NBE Risk Management Guidelines expect
  3. 3.The three lines model
  4. 4.The risk management process
  5. 5.Rating likelihood and impact
  6. 6.Building the branch risk register
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe the roles of the board, board risk committee, CRO and ALCO in an Ethiopian bank
  • Explain the three lines model and your own role in it
  • Apply the risk process: identify, assess, control, monitor and report
  • Rate risks by likelihood and impact and record them in a risk register
