ERM frameworks, risk appetite and limits

Module 04 / 5 · 65 min

Design or review an enterprise risk management framework, write a risk appetite statement and cascade it into limits with clear breach rules.

In this module

  1. 1.COSO, ISO 31000 and the Basel principles
  2. 2.Integrating ERM with strategy
  3. 3.The risk appetite statement
  4. 4.Setting the metrics
  5. 5.Cascading limits
  6. 6.Managing limit breaches
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Compare COSO ERM, ISO 31000 and the Basel Core Principles and relate them to NBE expectations
  • Integrate risk into strategy and business planning
  • Draft a risk appetite statement with capacity, appetite, tolerance and limits
  • Cascade limits from the board to business lines and branches
  • Manage limit breaches with clear escalation
