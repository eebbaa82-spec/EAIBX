Measuring, stress testing and reporting risk

Module 05 / 5 · 70 min

Use RCSA, KRIs, basic VaR and a combined stress test, feed the results into capital and recovery planning, and report clearly to the board and the NBE.

In this module

  1. 1.Risk and control self-assessment
  2. 2.Basic value at risk
  3. 3.Designing the combined stress scenario
  4. 4.Running the numbers
  5. 5.Capital and recovery planning
  6. 6.Reporting to the board and the NBE
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Run a risk and control self-assessment and compare inherent and residual risk
  • Compute and interpret a basic one-day and ten-day VaR
  • Design and run a combined depreciation, drought and regional conflict stress test
  • Link stress results to capital planning and SBB/93/2025 recovery plans
  • Present a clear risk report to a board risk committee and act on findings
