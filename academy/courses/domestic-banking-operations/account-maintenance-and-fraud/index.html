Account maintenance, fraud and reporting

Module 05 / 5 · 60 min

Maintain accounts correctly, control suspense items, recognize and respond to fraud and forgery, and report to the right authority while treating customers fairly.

In this module

  1. 1.Inactive and dormant accounts
  2. 2.Closing accounts, signatures, mandates and powers of attorney
  3. 3.Suspense accounts
  4. 4.Fraud and forgery: what to look for
  5. 5.Digital fraud and customer awareness
  6. 6.Reporting, freezing and recording losses
  7. 7.Fair treatment and complaints
  8. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Handle inactive and dormant accounts, closures, signature changes and powers of attorney
  • Follow up and clear suspense items on time
  • Recognize forgery, insider collusion and digital fraud and prevent it
  • Report suspicious transactions and operational losses, and know the NBE's power to freeze funds
  • Treat customers fairly and handle complaints

  • Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021, Proclamation No. 780/2013 and Civil Society Organizations Proclamation No. 1113/2019
