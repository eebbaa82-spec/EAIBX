Customer and Stakeholder Focus

Module 05 / 6 · 55 min

See who your internal and external customers are, what they expect, and how service standards and good complaint handling turn everyday work into loyalty.

In this module

  1. 1.Who your customers and stakeholders are
  2. 2.Internal customers
  3. 3.External customers and what they expect
  4. 4.Service excellence
  5. 5.Handling complaints

In this module you will learn to

  • Identify your internal and external customers and other stakeholders
  • Describe what external customers expect and the value of keeping them
  • Apply simple service standards and handle a complaint step by step
  • Represent the company fairly to suppliers, inspectors and the community
