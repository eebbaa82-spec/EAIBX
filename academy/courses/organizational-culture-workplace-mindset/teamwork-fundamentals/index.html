Teamwork Fundamentals

Module 04 / 6 · 55 min

Work well with others: collaborate across shifts and departments, treat every colleague with respect and inclusion, and stop small frictions before they become conflicts.

In this module

  1. 1.What makes a team work
  2. 2.Collaboration and handovers
  3. 3.Clear roles and shared responsibility
  4. 4.Respect and inclusion
  5. 5.Preventing conflict
  6. 6.Being a good teammate every day
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Describe what makes a team effective and your role in it
  • Use handovers, shared information and clear roles to collaborate across teams
  • Practice respect and inclusion with colleagues of different backgrounds, faiths, ages and abilities
  • Recognize early signs of conflict and use simple habits to prevent it
