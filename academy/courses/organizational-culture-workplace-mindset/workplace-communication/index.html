Workplace Communication Skills

Module 03 / 6 · 55 min

Communicate clearly in person, on the phone and in writing, listen so that instructions are understood, and handle meetings and difficult interactions professionally.

In this module

  1. 1.Professional communication
  2. 2.Writing clear messages and emails
  3. 3.Listening skills
  4. 4.Asking questions and raising concerns respectfully
  5. 5.Managing meetings and everyday interactions
  6. 6.Handling difficult interactions
  7. ✓Module quiz

In this module you will learn to

  • Choose the right channel and write clear, courteous messages and emails
  • Use active listening and confirm instructions by summarizing them back
  • Raise a concern or disagree with a senior respectfully
  • Run or join a short, purposeful meeting and handle a difficult conversation calmly
