Financial Literacy for Everyone

Ten short, illustrated modules on running a business, setting goals, budgeting, saving, borrowing and staying safe with money. Free and open to everyone: register once with your name and contact details to start.

How the course works

  1. 1

    Learn in small steps

    Each module has short lessons with pictures you can read in a few minutes.

  2. 2

    See it, then try it

    Animated graphics show each idea, and real-life examples show it at work.

  3. 3

    Check what you learned

    A short quiz ends each module. Your progress is saved on this device.

The ten modules

  1. Module 01Introduction to Business and EntrepreneurshipLearn what a business is, what makes an entrepreneur, and how to plan a business on paper.5 lessons · 12 min
  2. Module 02Goal SettingTurn your dreams into clear financial goals with a timeframe, a plan, and people who support you.7 lessons · 14 min
  3. Module 03BudgetingLearn to plan your money with a budget and stay within it.7 lessons · 12 min
  4. Module 04Basics of BookkeepingLearn to record your business money, read simple reports and manage your taxes.7 lessons · 15 min
  5. Module 05SavingsLearn why saving matters, where to save, and how to make a savings plan.6 lessons · 10 min
  6. Module 06InvestmentLearn what investing is, the main types, what to check before you invest, and how to spread your risk.5 lessons · 7 min
  7. Module 07Sources of FinanceLearn where business money can come from, and how to borrow and manage a loan responsibly.7 lessons · 14 min
  8. Module 08Financial ServicesLearn who provides financial services, how to open an account, and the pros and cons of formal and informal finance.7 lessons · 13 min
  9. Module 09Digital Financial ServicesLearn how phones, cards, ATMs and POS devices help you manage money, and how to use them safely.7 lessons · 12 min
  10. Module 10Risk Management and InsuranceLearn to spot the risks to your life and business, handle them step by step, and use insurance and retirement planning.7 lessons · 12 min
