Goal Setting

Turn your dreams into clear financial goals with a timeframe, a plan, and people who support you.

ይህን ኮርስ በአማርኛ ያንብቡ

In this module

  1. 1.Dreams and goals
  2. 2.Financial goals
  3. 3.Short, medium, and long term
  4. 4.Managing your money
  5. 5.Why some people miss their goals
  6. 6.Map your route to success
  7. 7.SMARTEST goals in ten steps
  8. ✓Check your understanding

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain the reasons for setting goals.
  • Tell the difference between short-term, medium-term, and long-term goals.
  • Set financial goals and explain how to reach them.
  • Describe what a financial plan is and how it helps your financial wellbeing.
