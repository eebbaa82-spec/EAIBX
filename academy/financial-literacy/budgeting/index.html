Budgeting

Learn to plan your money with a budget and stay within it.

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what a budget is.
  • Describe why a realistic budget helps you and your business.
  • Follow the steps to create a budget.
  • Use a budget to manage your business well.
  • Explain why it matters to stay within your budget.
