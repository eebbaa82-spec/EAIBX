Sources of Finance

Learn where business money can come from, and how to borrow and manage a loan responsibly.

ይህን ኮርስ በአማርኛ ያንብቡ

In this module

  1. 1.Where business money comes from
  2. 2.Sharia-compliant (interest-free) financing
  3. 3.Why borrow? Debt financing
  4. 4.Planning and applying for a loan
  5. 5.Using and monitoring your loan
  6. 6.Lease financing
  7. 7.Finance for growing or going public
  8. ✓Check your understanding

In this module you will learn to

  • Name the main sources of finance for a business
  • Weigh the advantages and disadvantages of different financing options
  • Understand the steps and documents needed to get a loan
  • Use and monitor a loan wisely, by the agreed terms
