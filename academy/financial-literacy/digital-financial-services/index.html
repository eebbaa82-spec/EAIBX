Digital Financial Services

Learn how phones, cards, ATMs and POS devices help you manage money, and how to use them safely.

ይህን ኮርስ በአማርኛ ያንብቡ

In this module

  1. 1.What is digital finance?
  2. 2.Channels: how you reach your bank
  3. 3.Fintech and types of digital services
  4. 4.Withdrawing cash from an ATM
  5. 5.How POS helps small businesses
  6. 6.Benefits of digital financial services
  7. 7.Challenges and how to overcome them
  8. ✓Check your understanding

In this module you will learn to

  • Explain what digital finance and digital financial services are
  • Tell the difference between self-service and over-the-counter channels
  • Name common digital services, such as mobile money, ATM and POS
  • Withdraw cash from an ATM step by step
  • Describe the benefits and challenges of digital finance
