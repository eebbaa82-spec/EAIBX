School of Professional Skills

Cross-cutting skills that strengthen every role in a modern financial institution or growing business.

  • FoundationalCorporate Governance and EthicsLearn who directs and checks an Ethiopian bank or company under SBB/91/2024, SBB/89/2024, SBB/88/2024, the Commercial Code and capital market rules, then work through four real-life dilemma cards on loans, gifts, data and kickbacks.5 modules · 30 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Foundational to IntermediateRisk Management FrameworkLearn how Ethiopian banks manage credit, market, liquidity, operational, compliance, cyber, strategic and climate risk, then draft a risk appetite statement and a combined stress test for a mid-sized bank.5 modules · 30 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • FoundationalSustainable Finance and ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance)A practical introduction to sustainable finance for Ethiopian bank staff, from double materiality and drought risk to exclusion lists, E&S categories, green lending and greenwashing, built around screening ten real-looking loan applications.5 modules · 30 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • FoundationalData Analytics and Digital LiteracyBuild the everyday digital and data skills finance staff need: secure use of digital tools, cleaning and summarizing data in Excel, and handling customer data lawfully, ending with a branch dashboard you build yourself.5 modules · 30 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • FoundationalGender-Sensitive Financial ServicesLearn why women are underserved in Ethiopian finance, how to serve them without bias, and which products, channels and collateral alternatives fit their needs, using a woman-owned café in Adama as the running case.5 modules · 32 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • FoundationalDigital Marketing and Digital BankingLearn the digital funnel, Ethiopia's main channels, clear and compliant content in local languages, and how to drive real use of digital banking, ending with a campus content plan.5 modules · 26 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • FoundationalSocial Media ManagementLearn how an Ethiopian bank or microfinance institution runs its official social media day to day: planning and approving posts, answering comments and complaints, moving private matters to secure channels, fighting fake pages and scams, and following the staff social media policy, ending with a 15-post feed simulation.5 modules · 31 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • IntermediateStrategic Leadership and Change ManagementRead Ethiopia's fast-changing financial landscape, cascade strategy into KPIs and lead large change with people on board, using a two-bank merger as the running case.5 modules · 30 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Beginner · CertificateOrganizational Culture for Employees: Building the Right Workplace MindsetLearn how culture shapes every workplace and how your daily behavior builds it, then practice conduct, communication, teamwork, service and accountability through a first quarter at Sheba Fresh Foods PLC and finish with your own 90-day commitments plan.6 modules · 36 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Intermediate · Professional CertificateOrganizational Culture for Employees: High-Performance Workplace BehaviorsBuild a team climate where people speak up, resolve conflict, improve every week, adapt to change and stay calm under pressure, following one Ethiopian distribution team through a quarter and finishing with your own team climate review.6 modules · 35 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Advanced · Executive CertificateOrganizational Culture for Employees: Culture Champions and Workplace LeadersLearn to influence without a title, build habits of excellence, keep values and ethics alive, lead inclusive teams and coach others, then turn a culture diagnosis into a measured 90-day Culture Improvement Action Plan.6 modules · 36 lessons · final exam · certificate
