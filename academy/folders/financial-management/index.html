Taxation Program

Ethiopian business taxation from registration and filing to tax planning, transfer pricing and audits, on the current proclamations and directives.

  • Beginner · CertificateTaxation Fundamentals for Ethiopian Businesses: Tax Awareness and Compliance EssentialsLearn the Ethiopian tax system as it stands after the 2024 to 2026 reforms, then register, invoice, compute VAT, PAYE and pension, and keep a compliance calendar through a month as accounts and tax assistant at Haro Furniture and Interiors PLC, and build your own tax compliance file.6 modules · 32 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Intermediate · Professional CertificateTaxation Fundamentals for Ethiopian Businesses: Business Tax ManagementCompute business income tax under the 2025 rules, run withholding and VAT correctly, reconcile tax to the books and build an audit-ready tax file, following a packaging manufacturer through its quarter-end and a Ministry of Revenues audit.6 modules · 37 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Advanced · Executive CertificateTaxation Fundamentals for Ethiopian Businesses: Strategic Tax ManagementLearn to cut a company's tax bill only in ways the law allows: model the 2.5% minimum alternative tax and the new incentive regime under Regulation No. 586/2026, run tax health checks and acquisition due diligence, compare debt, equity and leasing after tax, manage permanent establishment, treaty and transfer pricing rules, and structure acquisitions and reorganizations, then build a tax strategy and due diligence report for a growing Ethiopian group.6 modules · 38 lessons · final exam · certificate

SME Financial Management Program

Setting up a company, keeping Excel-based records, monitoring performance and preparing an investor-ready business.

  • Beginner · CertificateFinancial Management for SMEs: Financial Literacy for SMEsRegister and set up your business finances the right way, learn the basics of money flow and bookkeeping, then build and use a complete Excel bookkeeping workbook for one month at Bereket Printing and Stationery PLC: daily sales, income and expense trackers, cash, bank and petty cash, and a monthly review.6 modules · 37 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Intermediate · Professional CertificateFinancial Management for SMEs: Financial Control and Decision MakingTake control of a growing business's money: forecast cash 13 weeks ahead, prepare IFRS for SMEs statements, automate reports and dashboards in Excel, run budgets and variance analysis, control stock and assets, and read the ratios a bank will read. You follow one Kombolcha flour and pasta maker through a tight quarter and finish with your own monthly management dashboard.6 modules · 36 lessons · final exam · certificate
  • Advanced · Executive CertificateFinancial Management for SMEs: Strategic Financial ManagementBuild a three-year financial model with scenarios, prepare investor-ready financials, compare bank loans, equity, leasing and development finance, manage credit, liquidity, FX and fraud risk, and run the business on a KPI dashboard and balanced scorecard.6 modules · 36 lessons · final exam · certificate
