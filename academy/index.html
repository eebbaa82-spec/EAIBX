For banks and institutions: Enterprise subscriptions

Train and certify your whole team

Banks, MFIs and other institutions can subscribe on behalf of their staff. Give employees access to the full catalog, assign learning paths by role, and track progress and certification from one dashboard.

  • Role-based learning paths. Assign the right credit, treasury, trade finance or capital markets path to each team.

  • Training department dashboard. Give staff their seat codes, assign courses and due dates, and see enrollment, completion, exam and simulation scores and certificates by branch, department or employee.

  • Blended delivery. Combine self-paced online courses with live virtual classes or on-site workshops for your staff.

  • Custom content. Add your institution's own policies and procedures alongside our courses.

  • Simple billing. One annual invoice in ETB, priced per seat.
