About Erudite Africa Connect

We are building the exchange Ethiopian business has been waiting for.

Ethiopia has one of Africa's fastest-growing economies, a young and ambitious generation of founders, and thousands of established businesses with real value. What it has lacked is one trusted place where ideas meet capital, where good businesses can find buyers, and where struggling businesses can find a way back. Erudite Africa Connect is that place.

Our story

Erudite Africa Connect, Erudite Africa's business and investment platform, was founded by Erudite Africa Investments PLC to close the gap between Ethiopian enterprise and the capital, skills and networks it needs.

Erudite Africa was established in May 2020 by Befikadu Eba, a corporate banker and macro-advisory professional with over 16 years of experience in capital allocation, risk assessment and financial inclusion. It began as an advisory practice working closely with investors, regulators and financial institutions, and in February 2026 it was incorporated as Erudite Africa Investments PLC, with a management team of specialist division heads.

Through that work, the firm saw the same gaps again and again: good businesses that could not reach investors, investors who could not find reliable information, and struggling businesses that asked for help too late. Erudite Africa Connect brings Erudite Africa's investment facilitation, turnaround, market intelligence and training expertise onto one platform, so many more businesses can reach it.

Mission

To help Ethiopian and African businesses start, grow, raise capital and recover, by connecting them with investors, experts and knowledge on one trusted platform.

Vision

A continent where every viable business can find the capital and support it needs, and no good business fails for lack of a second chance.

What we do

Grow.

Incubation, acceleration and mentorship that turn ideas into investable companies.

Invest.

A curated marketplace and secure deal rooms that connect verified businesses with local and international investors.

Recover.

Business diagnostics, turnaround advisory, bank and MFI credit relationship support and a responsible exchange for distressed assets.
