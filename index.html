How Investopia works

  1. 1

    Create your profile.

    Tell us who you are and what you need. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. 2

    Get diagnosed.

    Our Enterprise Health & Investment Readiness Assessment (EHIRA) scores your business across eight dimensions, from finance and operations to governance and investment readiness, with grades A to E.

  3. 3

    Get matched.

    Investopia introduces you to the right programs, mentors, advisors, investors or buyers based on your profile and score.

  4. 4

    Close and grow.

    Share documents in a secure deal room, agree terms with expert support, and keep building with the Academy.
