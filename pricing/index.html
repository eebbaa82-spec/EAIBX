Pricing FAQ

Can I cancel any time?+
Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
Do you take a share of my business?+
Not by joining or using Erudite Africa Connect. Erudite Africa Connect takes equity in a business only when this is agreed in writing in advance with the business and its investors, as payment for advisory or capital-raising services.
Is there a discount for startups in programs?+
Yes. Program participants get one month of the Growth plan free.
Can I pay from abroad?+
Yes. Diaspora investors can pay by international card on request. Contact us and we will set up payment for you.
