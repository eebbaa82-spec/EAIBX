How EHIRA works

The dimensions, weights, grades and red flags behind the Enterprise Health & Investment Readiness Assessment.

Eight dimensions

DimensionWhat we look at
Financial HealthFinancial statements and audit, profitability, liquidity, leverage, debt service, cash reserves and loan arrears
Market & CustomerCustomer base, retention, satisfaction, customer concentration, written contracts and traceable payments
Operational ExcellenceOn-time delivery, procedures, quality, dependence on imported inputs and on a single supplier
Human Capital & LeadershipStaff turnover, training, succession, pension registration and employment contracts
Governance & ComplianceBoard, internal controls, tax clearance, IFRS reporting and up-to-date company records
Strategic ManagementStrategic plan, monthly KPIs, budgeting and, for businesses under pressure, a turnaround plan
Technology ReadinessAccounting or ERP systems, cybersecurity and system-issued invoices
Growth & Investment ReadinessBusiness plan, financial model, pitch deck, funding needs, credit record and collateral

What the grades mean

GradeScoreMeaning
A90 to 100Investment Ready. Strong governance, strong profitability and low risk.
B80 to 89Healthy. Good systems with minor weaknesses.
C70 to 79Stable but Needs Improvement. Average management and some operational issues.
D60 to 69Vulnerable. Financing challenges and weak controls.
EBelow 60Critical. Significant risk. Immediate intervention required.

Some answers cap the grade however high the score: for example, no annual financial statements or an expired tax clearance certificate limit the grade to C, and negative equity, debt service coverage below 1.0 or a loan more than 90 days overdue limit it to D.

After scoring, Investopia lists the key gaps holding back each index below 80 and the actions that close them, such as obtaining a tax clearance certificate, registering movable assets in the collateral registry or preparing an investment memorandum.

Two levels

Free self-assessment

Take the quick self-check (selected questions, under 5 minutes) or the full assessment (about 20 minutes) and see your indicative grade, readiness scores and actions on screen. Downloadable reports are available with payment.

Verified EHIRA (price announced after the launch period)

Upload your financial statements, tax clearance certificate and supporting documents. An Investopia analyst reviews them and issues a verified grade that investors and lenders can rely on, valid for 12 months.

EHIRA is a decision-support and readiness tool. It does not replace a credit rating, an audit, legal due diligence or a lender's decision.
