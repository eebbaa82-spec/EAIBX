One platform for the whole life of a business

EABIX is Ethiopia's first complete business and investment exchange. It connects entrepreneurs, business owners, investors, lenders, mentors and advisors in one trusted place.

Grow

Incubation and acceleration programs, mentor matching and the EABIX Academy for founders and finance professionals.

Invest

An Opportunities Marketplace and curated Deal Rooms of diagnosed, graded businesses, with secure document sharing.

Recover

The Business Health Check scores businesses from A to E across finance, operations, market, people and governance, followed by guided recovery support.

Lend with confidence

The Credit Management Center helps businesses manage bank and MFI relationships and helps lenders complete client due diligence faster.
