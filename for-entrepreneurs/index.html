How it works

  1. 1

    Join free and build your profile.

    Add your business basics, team, traction and what you are looking for.

  2. 2

    Take the EHIRA assessment.

    Get an independent A to E grade and a short report on your strengths and gaps.

  3. 3

    Choose your route.

    Apply to a program, request a mentor, list for investment, or list your business for sale.

  4. 4

    Meet the right people.

    We match you with investors, buyers, mentors and advisors. You decide who sees what.

  5. 5

    Close the deal.

    Share documents in a secure deal room and get expert help with valuation, terms and due diligence.

Benefits

  • Stay confidential. Your listing shows an anonymous teaser until you approve each investor or buyer.

  • Be taken seriously. A verified health grade tells investors you have nothing to hide.

  • Get better, not just funded. Every gap in your report links to a course, a mentor or an advisor who can fix it.
