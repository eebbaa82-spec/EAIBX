Entrepreneurs and business owners

Raise capital, grow faster or sell on your terms.

Whether you have an idea on paper or a business with twenty years of history, Erudite Africa Connect gives you a clear path to the next stage.

How it works

  1. 1

    Join free and build your profile.

    Add your business basics, team, traction and what you are looking for.

  2. 2

    Take the EHIRA assessment.

    Get an independent A to E grade and a short report on your strengths and gaps.

  3. 3

    Choose your route.

    Apply to a program, request a mentor, list for investment, or list your business for sale.

  4. 4

    Meet the right people.

    We match you with investors, buyers, mentors and advisors. You decide who sees what.

  5. 5

    Close the deal.

    Share documents in a secure deal room and get expert help with valuation, terms and due diligence.

Benefits

  • Stay confidential. Your listing shows an anonymous teaser until you approve each investor or buyer.

  • Be taken seriously. A verified health grade tells investors you have nothing to hide.

  • Get better, not just funded. Every gap in your report links to a program, a mentor or an advisor who can help fix it.

Investors and buyers

Verified Ethiopian deal flow, without the noise.

Erudite Africa Connect helps angel investors, funds, family offices, corporates and diaspora investors find, assess and close opportunities with confidence.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create your investor profile.

    Set your sectors, ticket size, stage and regions of interest.

  2. 2

    Get verified.

    Complete our investor verification so businesses know you are genuine.

  3. 3

    Receive matched opportunities.

    Get alerts for businesses that fit your mandate, each with a health grade and summary.

  4. 4

    Request deal room access.

    Review financials, legal documents and management Q&A in a secure, watermarked space.

  5. 5

    Invest or acquire.

    Agree terms directly or with an advisor listed on Erudite Africa Connect, and track your portfolio on your dashboard.

Benefits

  • Graded, not guessed. Every listed business carries an independent A to E health grade.

  • Curated deal rooms. Themed showcases bring the strongest businesses in a sector to you at once.

  • Local insight. Market reports, sector briefs and on-the-ground advisors reduce the cost of investing in Ethiopia.

  • Diaspora friendly. Invest from abroad with clear guidance on Ethiopian rules and processes. Our team confirms what applies to you before you commit.
