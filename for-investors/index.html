How it works

  1. 1

    Create your investor profile.

    Set your sectors, ticket size, stage and regions of interest.

  2. 2

    Get verified.

    Complete our investor verification so businesses know you are genuine.

  3. 3

    Receive matched opportunities.

    Get alerts for businesses that fit your mandate, each with a health grade and summary.

  4. 4

    Request deal room access.

    Review financials, legal documents and management Q&A in a secure, watermarked space.

  5. 5

    Invest or acquire.

    Agree terms directly or with an Investopia-listed advisor, and track your portfolio on your dashboard.

Benefits

  • Graded, not guessed. Every listed business carries an independent A to E health grade.

  • Curated deal rooms. Themed showcases bring the strongest businesses in a sector to you at once.

  • Local insight. Market reports, sector briefs and on-the-ground advisors reduce the cost of investing in Ethiopia.

  • Diaspora friendly. Invest from abroad with clear guidance on Ethiopian rules and processes. Our team confirms what applies to you before you commit.
